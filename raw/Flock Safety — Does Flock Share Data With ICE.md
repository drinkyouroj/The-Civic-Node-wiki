# Does Flock Share Data With ICE?

**Primary assertion:** "No. Flock does not work with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) or any other sub-agency of the Department of Homeland Security."

**Contractual status:** "Flock does not have a contract with ICE or any sub-agency of the Department of Homeland Security."

**Data control model:** "Every piece of data collected by Flock license plate readers is owned and controlled by the customer." Local agencies retain decision-making authority over data sharing without Flock override capability. Agencies are contractually prohibited from using Flock data for immigration enforcement.

## Timeline of Federal Access Safeguards (2024–2026)

- **June 2024:** Illinois policy attestation implemented
- **March 2025:** National Lookup disabled for California agencies
- **June 2025:** Automatic keyword blocks deployed for immigration and reproductive-health searches; federal agencies blocked from creating new California relationships
- **July 2025:** Virginia restrictions enacted; platform-wide enhancements including case number requirements and custom search filters
- **August 2025:** Structural reorganization — federal agencies separated into distinct organization type with restricted access; removed from statewide/national lookup networks
- **January 2026:** New toggle enabling agencies to disable all federal sharing
