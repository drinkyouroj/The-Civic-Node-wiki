Reference compilation on the legal/procedural framework for DOJ→agency-IG handoffs and IG→DOJ criminal referrals.

## Statutory framework
Inspectors general are required to "report expeditiously to the Attorney General whenever the Inspector General has reasonable grounds to believe there has been a violation of criminal law" (Inspector General Act). Referrals normally go to the U.S. Attorney in the district where the conduct occurred.

DOJ Justice Manual §934 (Policy Statement on DOJ Relationship and Coordination with Statutory Inspectors General) governs coordination — establishes that OIG investigations finding potential criminal misconduct or civil liability are referred to the appropriate prosecutorial office.

## Direction matters
The standard, well-precedented direction is **IG → DOJ**: an IG audit/investigation surfaces possible criminal conduct, IG refers to DOJ, DOJ decides whether to charge.

The **DOJ → IG** direction (as in Powell case — DOJ closes, hands off to IG) is less commonly framed this way in public reporting. It is closer to a deferral or non-prosecution disposition where the agency's IG continues administrative oversight. The reopen condition Tillis flagged ("only a criminal referral from the IG would reopen") effectively places the IG as the gatekeeper for any future DOJ action.

## Recent precedent for IG-led criminal pathways into independent agencies
- NARA: Feb 9, 2022, NARA's OIG made a referral to DOJ that triggered the Mar-a-Lago documents investigation. Demonstrates IG-to-DOJ referrals as the established channel for independent-agency criminal probes.
- Andrew McCabe (FBI): DOJ OIG referred McCabe to federal prosecutors for false statements (case ultimately not charged) — IG-as-criminal-referral-source model.

## How unusual is the Powell handoff?
The DOJ-closes-and-hands-to-IG sequence is procedurally available but politically conspicuous here because:
1. The criminal probe was opened first (without an IG referral to ground it), then closed under judicial pressure (Boasberg) and political pressure (Tillis hold).
2. The Fed IG had already audited the same renovation in 2021 and found no criminal conduct.
3. The reopen condition (IG criminal referral) is essentially a remote chance given (2), which is what allowed Tillis to take the deal.

The mechanism functions as a de-escalation pathway that lets DOJ retreat without admitting the case had no merit, while preserving rhetorical option to "restart."
