Sen. Thom Tillis (R-NC) announced he will lift his block on Kevin Warsh's confirmation as Fed chair after DOJ dropped its criminal probe of Jerome Powell on April 24. Tillis: "I am prepared to move on with the confirmation of Mr. Warsh. I think he's going to be a great Fed Chair."

Tillis had blocked the process over the DOJ probe related to Powell's congressional testimony about the Fed building renovation project. Once DOJ confirmed it would drop the case, Tillis reversed.

## Continuing IG track
The Federal Reserve's inspector general (Michael Horowitz) will continue reviewing Powell's handling of the renovation project — separately from the closed criminal investigation.

## Reopen trigger
Tillis received commitments from DOJ representatives that the criminal case would not reopen unless the IG issued a criminal referral.
