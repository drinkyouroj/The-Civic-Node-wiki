A gunman opened fire near the White House Saturday evening and was shot and killed by the U.S. Secret Service, the agency said.

A bystander also was struck by gunfire, according to the Secret Service. No officers were injured. President [Donald Trump](https://www.usatoday.com/news/politics/donald-trump/) was in the White House during the May 23 incident but was not impacted.

The shooting took place shortly after 6 p.m. EDT near 17th Street and Pennsylvania Avenue outside the White House complex on the west side. The gunman "pulled a weapon from his bag and began firing," according to the Secret Service statement. Officers returned fire, hitting the gunman who later was pronounced dead at the hospital.

The Secret Service did not release the condition of the bystander.

The suspected gunman is 21-year-old Nasire Best, according to an official familiar with the incident. Best, of Dundalk, Maryland, was [charged in DC Superior Court with attempting to unlawfully enter](https://eaccess.dccourts.gov/eaccess/search.page.3.1?x=aCxGvLV0O5lXRhYUrTZBh0kPV2uJ67JU9Pp3Q4bfn7ti76dmLuT*ybwXRj5e5hcqxLgXHldqA8WfCLAJyLsiE79kHSVJNdht1kig*L-BVa-GLe7sBJa8r520vvUXdNiallOZNZmEJuxw-cebOD8mYkFb48VxI8SmsqxmEebZnH0) 1699 State Place NW, one of the entry points to the White House complex, on July 10, 2025.

The incident outside the White House Saturday sent reporters who were working nearby scrambling, and prompted a multi-agency law enforcement response. Reporters said they heard what appeared to be dozens of gunshots.

## Alleged gunman, 21, identified in shooting

The gunman killed by Secret Service agents outside the White House is Nasire Best, 21, according to an official familiar with the incident.

Best, of Dundalk, Maryland, was [charged in DC Superior Court with attempting to unlawfully enter](https://eaccess.dccourts.gov/eaccess/search.page.3.1?x=aCxGvLV0O5lXRhYUrTZBh0kPV2uJ67JU9Pp3Q4bfn7ti76dmLuT*ybwXRj5e5hcqxLgXHldqA8WfCLAJyLsiE79kHSVJNdht1kig*L-BVa-GLe7sBJa8r520vvUXdNiallOZNZmEJuxw-cebOD8mYkFb48VxI8SmsqxmEebZnH0) 1699 State Place NW, one of the entry points to the White House complex, on July 10, 2025.

A judge issued a “stay-away” order to Best while the case was pending on July 11, 2025, according to court records. A warrant was issued for Best’s arrest on Aug. 7, 2025, for failing to appear in the case.

The incident happened about 5:30 p.m., when a uniformed DC police officer was staffing the pedestrian access to the White House campus. Best allegedly walked into a restricted area through an exit turnstile that was marked “Do Not Enter Exit Only,” [according to court records.](https://eaccess.dccourts.gov/eaccess/search.page.3.1?x=aCxGvLV0O5lXRhYUrTZBh0kPV2uJ67JU9Pp3Q4bfn7ti76dmLuT*ybwXRj5e5hcqxLgXHldqA8WfCLAJyLsiE79kHSVJNdht1kig*L-BVa-GLe7sBJa8r18sV8EutdK395xf9JxDVn0b4Unw8N5VHhPeK7Mym6nWbpCfob6AduU)

Best allegedly said he was Jesus Christ and wanted to be arrested, according to a police affidavit. The Secret Service was familiar with Best allegedly “walking around the White House complex inquiring how to gain access at various entry posts,” the affidavit said.

Best was involuntarily committed on June 26, 2025, by the Secret Service for allegedly obstructing vehicle entry to the White House complex at 15th Street and E Street.

## Secret Service: Trump was inside White House during shooting

President Donald Trump was inside the White House during the shooting incident on Saturday evening, according to a U.S. Secret Service [post on X](https://x.com/SecretSvcSpox/status/2058341552234115202?s=20).

The Secret Service said the president was confirmed to be in the building at the time, but added that no protectees were harmed and operations were not affected.

Earlier in the day, Trump posted that he was inside the Oval Office as he focused on [international negotiations](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/world/2026/05/23/trump-says-peace-deal-to-end-war-with-iran-is-largely-negotiated--live/90226024007/), announcing that a deal to end the war between the United States and Iran had been “largely negotiated,” with final details still being worked out. The president said the agreement could be finalized soon after weeks of talks aimed at moving from a ceasefire toward a lasting peace.

## Sen. John Thune: 'Grateful for Secret Service' after White House shooting

Senate Majority Leader John Thune thanked the U.S. Secret Service after gunfire near the White House prompted a major security response Saturday evening.

In a [post on X](https://x.com/LeaderJohnThune/status/2058336474190528559?s=20), the South Dakota Republican said he was grateful for the “decisive actions” taken by Secret Service agents to protect President Donald Trump and others near the White House during the incident.

“Grateful for the Secret Service and the agents’ decisive actions to protect President Trump and everyone at and around the White House this evening,” Thune wrote.

Thune added that he and his wife, Kimberley, were praying “for the safety and security of those who were in harm’s way.”

## 2 injured in shooting near White House: reports

At least two people were injured Saturday evening after gunfire erupted near the White House, according to unnamed law enforcement officials cited by [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/white-house-gun-shots-north-lawn/) and [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/gunshots-reported-near-white-house-rcna346671).

Law enforcement sources told CBS News that two people were wounded, including a suspect, after approximately 15 to 30 shots were fired near 17th Street and Pennsylvania Avenue NW.

Officials said one of the injured individuals is believed to be the suspect, though it was not immediately clear whether the second person was a bystander or also connected to the shooting.

Their conditions were not immediately known.

U.S. Secret Service agents engaged with the suspect and fired their weapons during the confrontation, according to NBC News. Officials also said there did not appear to be any injuries to Secret Service personnel.

USA TODAY has reached out to the Secret Service for comment.

## Sen. Rick Scott praises Secret Service after White House shooting

Republican Florida Sen. Rick Scott praised Secret Service agents and called for an end to political violence after gunfire erupted near the White House on Saturday evening.

In a [post on X](https://x.com/SenRickScott/status/2058326058454204603?s=20), Scott said he was thankful that President Donald Trump, first lady Melania Trump and others at the White House were safe following the incident. He also thanked Secret Service agents for what he described as a swift response.

“THANK YOU to the heroic Secret Service agents who acted quickly and decisively to neutralize the threat,” Scott wrote.

The senator also called the incident “yet another attempt on the president’s life” and said the violence “needs to stop.” Scott added that “the chaos needs to end” and that “the rule of law MUST prevail.”

## ABC’s Selina Wang ducks for cover during White House scare

ABC News senior White House correspondent Selina Wang was reporting from the White House North Lawn on Saturday evening when the sound of gunfire suddenly interrupted her report.

Wang was discussing a [potential deal with Iran](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/world/2026/05/23/trump-says-peace-deal-to-end-war-with-iran-is-largely-negotiated--live/90226024007/) when several loud shots rang out in the distance. In video captured during the moment, a surprised look flashes across her face before she quickly ducks down to the ground.

The reporter is then seen crouching as she unhooked her iPhone from a recording stand while Secret Service agents respond nearby.

“I was in the middle of taping on my iPhone for a social video from the White House North Lawn when we heard the shots,” [Wang later wrote on X](https://x.com/selinawangtv/status/2058312115098697760?s=20). “It sounded like dozens of gunshots. We were told to sprint to the press briefing room where we are holding now.”
