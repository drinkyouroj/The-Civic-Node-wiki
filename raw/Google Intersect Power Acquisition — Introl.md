Introl Blog analysis of Alphabet/Google's $4.75 billion acquisition of Intersect Power (announced December 22, 2025), framed as a hyperscaler vertical-integration / behind-the-meter generation move.

Deal terms:
- $4.75 billion cash plus debt assumption
- Closing expected H1 2026
- Acquired assets:
  - 3.6 GW solar and wind capacity under development
  - 3.1 GWh battery storage in development
  - 2.2 GW operational solar
  - 2.4 GWh operational storage

Specific project — Quantum Clean Energy Project (Texas):
- 640 MW solar with 1.3 GWh battery storage
- Operation: late spring 2026
- Full completion: 2027

Grid bottleneck context (the article's stated reason for vertical integration):
- Northern Virginia: 7-year interconnection delays
- California: 9+ year interconnection delays
- ~2,300 GW of generation capacity in US interconnection queues
- Only 20% of 2000-2018 requests reached operation by end of 2023 (LBNL data)

Google's data center power consumption growth:
- 2020: 14.4 TWh
- 2024: 30.8 TWh (doubled)
- 2028 projected: >60 TWh

Competitive landscape (parallel hyperscaler vertical-integration moves):
- Microsoft: $10 billion deal with Brookfield for 10.5 GW capacity (2026-2030)
- Amazon: Talen nuclear agreement
- Meta: deals with three nuclear providers (Vistra, TerraPower, Oklo) — up to 6.6 GW by 2035

"Energy park" innovation pattern (per the article):
- Co-located generation eliminates 5% grid transmission losses
- Bypasses multi-year interconnection queues
- Enables phased expansion without repeated permitting cycles

Direct quotes:
- Sheldon Kimber, Intersect CEO: "The logical extension of Intersect's partnership with Google."
- Amanda Peterson Corio, Google Global Head of Data Center Energy: "We're looking at siting next to power generation and creating these industrial parks."
- Google Sustainability Report: "Transmission barriers are the number one challenge we're seeing on the grid."

Strategic thesis: hyperscalers are shifting from PPAs toward vertical integration / behind-the-meter generation as a workaround for interconnection-queue depth.
