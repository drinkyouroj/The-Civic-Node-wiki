# Minnesota Prosecutors Charge ICE Agent with Assault

**Officer and Incident:**
Gregory Donnell Morgan, an ICE agent, was charged with two felony counts of second-degree assault after allegedly pointing his gun at two drivers while operating an unmarked SUV on February 5, 2026, in Minnesota.

**Charges and Penalty:**
Morgan faces up to 36 months in prison on the assault charges. Hennepin County Attorney Mary Moriarty announced the charges, describing it as "the first criminal case against a federal immigration officer involved in President Donald Trump's immigration campaign in the Twin Cities."

**Prosecutor Statement:**
Moriarty stated that the charges reflect "an effort to seek accountability for the harms inflicted on our community during Operation Metro Surge," adding that she believes this represents "the first case of its kind across the country."

**How Charges Were Filed:**
The victims called 911 following the encounter, triggering an investigation that led to charges. The article does not detail specific methods prosecutors used to overcome any federal obstruction.

**Government Response:**
The Department of Homeland Security did not immediately respond to requests for comment. No Trump administration reaction was included in the article.

**Related Context:**
Morgan participated in Operation Metro Surge, which detained over 3,700 people. The operation drew national attention following deaths and shootings involving other federal agents.
