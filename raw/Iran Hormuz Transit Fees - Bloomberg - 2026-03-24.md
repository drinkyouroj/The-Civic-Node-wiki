Iran has begun imposing informal transit fees of up to $2 million per vessel for safe passage through the Strait of Hormuz, on a case-by-case basis with no official announcement. Payment mechanism not yet clear at time of publication. Bloomberg sourced the report to anonymous sources familiar with the matter; Iran's foreign ministry declined to comment. An Iranian parliament member proposed legislation to legalize and formalize the fees.
