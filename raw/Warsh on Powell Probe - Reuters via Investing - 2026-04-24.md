# Key extracted quote (from web search summary, Reuters wire)

> "Warsh didn't immediately respond to a request for comment on the Justice Department's decision to drop its criminal investigation into Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell."

The Federal Reserve also declined to comment.

This is the closest the public record comes to an on-the-record Warsh response to the DOJ probe, and it is a non-response: Reuters sought comment on the day the probe was dropped (April 24, 2026) and Warsh's camp did not reply.

## Significance
- Probe opened January 2026 (concurrent with Warsh's nomination announcement Jan 30, 2026)
- Probe closed April 24, 2026
- Across that ~3-month window, no public statement by Warsh on the probe has surfaced in:
  - Hoover Institution publications (his Hoover output in this period focused on "Inflation Is A Choice" / Fed framework reform, not Powell)
  - Senate hearing testimony (April 21) — deflected related questions
  - Post-decision press requests (April 24) — did not respond
