# Decoding the Warsh Testimony

## Treasury-Fed Coordination Framework
The piece identifies a coordinated policy approach involving Kevin Warsh (Fed chair nominee), Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, and Fed Governor Stephen Miran. The author characterizes this as a strategic realignment rather than independent institution action.

## Key quotes / claims
- "Warsh, Bessent, and Miran are coordinating a managed dollar decline to rebalance global trade." (author's framing, not a sourced quote from any of the three)
- Warsh called the large Fed balance sheet "fiscal policy in disguise" and "explicitly wants to work with the Treasury Secretary to coordinate a reduction."
- Warsh in testimony: the Fed is "independent inside of government, not independent of government."
- The author interprets the framework as "the FX endgame" — a coordinated dollar weakening "to restore US trade competitiveness without sacrificing reserve currency status."

## Editorial weight
Capital Flows is a markets-analyst Substack, not a primary news outlet. The "coordination" claim here is interpretive, drawn from the public statements of all three plus the Miran dissent record. There is no leaked memo or readout cited. But the read is consistent with how multiple desk analysts (Krishna Guha, Tim Duy, Deutsche Bank rates) are now framing the Warsh proposal.
