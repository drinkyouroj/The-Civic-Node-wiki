SANTA CLARA -- For the second time in franchise history, the [Seattle Seahawks](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/sea/seattle-seahawks) are Super Bowl champions.

Led by their defense and Super Bowl MVP running back [Kenneth Walker III](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4567048/kenneth-walker-iii), the Seahawks stymied the [New England Patriots](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/ne/new-england-patriots) [29-13](https://www.espn.com/nfl/game/_/gameId/401772988/seahawks-patriots) in Super Bowl LX. Nicknamed the "Dark Side," the Seahawks' defense wreaked havoc on regular-season MVP runner-up quarterback [Drake Maye](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4431452/drake-maye), sacking him six times and forcing him to turn the ball over three times.

Seattle linebacker [Derick Hall](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4567226/derick-hall) forced the first turnover of the game -- his first since 2024 -- in the third quarter with a strip sack. In the fourth quarter, linebacker [Uchenna Nwosu](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/3120358/uchenna-nwosu) and safety [Julian Love](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4046675/julian-love) each snagged interceptions for the Seahawks.

The Patriots were held scoreless until Maye found wide receiver [Mack Hollins](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/2991662/mack-hollins) for a 35-yard touchdown to cut the lead to 19-7 with 12:27 remaining. But thanks to those back-to-back interceptions on the Patriots' next series, New England didn't get any closer.

Walker paced Seattle's offense with 135 rushing yards, averaging 5.0 yards per carry. And Seahawks quarterback [Sam Darnold](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/3912547/sam-darnold), a first-round-pick-turned-journeyman, had a turnover-free game and competed 19 of 38 passes for 202 yards and a touchdown.
