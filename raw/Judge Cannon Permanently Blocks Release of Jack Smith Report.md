# Judge Cannon Permanently Blocks Jack Smith Report in Trump Classified Documents Case

**Date:** February 23, 2026
**Location:** Fort Pierce, Florida
**Judge:** Aileen Cannon

## The Ruling

Judge Cannon rejected the Knight First Amendment Institute's effort to compel release of Special Counsel Jack Smith's report regarding President Trump's alleged unlawful retention of classified documents at Mar-a-Lago. The court also granted motions by Trump and former codefendants to permanently prohibit the report's release.

The decision permanently bars disclosure based largely on Judge Cannon's determination that Special Counsel Jack Smith was unlawfully appointed when preparing the report. The court denied defendants' request to order the report destroyed.

## Legal Arguments

The Knight Institute contended that suppression cannot be justified constitutionally or under common law. Scott Wilkens, senior counsel, stated: "There is no legitimate basis for its continued suppression."

Executive Director Jameel Jaffer emphasized: "The First Amendment protects the public's right of access to documents filed in connection with criminal trials."

## Appeal Status

The Knight Institute's appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit remains pending. The Institute filed this appeal earlier in February 2026, challenging Judge Cannon's earlier refusal to release the report and requesting reversal.
