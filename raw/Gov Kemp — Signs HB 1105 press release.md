# Gov. Kemp Signs Legislation Strengthening Public Safety and Supporting First Responders

**Date:** May 1, 2024 | **Source:** Office of the Governor, State of Georgia (gov.georgia.gov)

## HB 1105 Details

**Bill Sponsor:** Representative Jesse Petrea
**Co-signers:** Representatives J Collins, Josh Bonner, Emory Dunahoo, Matt Barton, Rick Jasperse
**Senate Carrier:** Senator John Albers
**Amendments:** Representative Houston Gaines
**Priority:** Lieutenant Governor Burt Jones and Speaker Jon Burns

## Bill Summary (per press release)

HB 1105 establishes procedures requiring jails to document the nationality and immigration status of confined individuals. The legislation mandates that the Department of Corrections and sheriffs submit reports detailing illegal immigrants in their custody.

## Signing Ceremony

The signing took place in Forsyth, GA, alongside First Lady Marty Kemp, Speaker Jon Burns, Attorney General Chris Carr, and other state leaders.

## Notes

This is Tier 1 primary source — official Georgia state government press release. Confirms HB 1105 signed May 1, 2024.
