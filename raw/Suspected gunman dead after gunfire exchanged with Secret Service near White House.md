U.S. Secret Service shot and killed a man after he opened fire near the White House on Saturday, according to a spokesperson for the agency.

The spokesperson [said in a statement](https://x.com/SecretSvcSpox/status/2058341552234115202/photo/1) that an individual approached the area near 17th Street and Pennsylvania Avenue NW shortly before 6 p.m. “pulled a weapon from his bag” and began firing.

Secret Service agents returned fire, hitting the suspect who was taken to a nearby hospital where he later died, the spokesperson said.

A bystander was also struck during the exchange; further details regarding their condition were not immediately available. There were no injuries reported to Secret Service officers.

President Trump was inside the White House at the time of the incident but was not impacted, the spokesperson noted.

Members of the media covering the president were rushed into a secure area inside the press briefing room after shots rang out, according to reporters with The Hill’s sister network, NewsNation. The press remained on lockdown for roughly 40 minutes.

DCNewsNow anchor Chris Flanagan, who was on the grounds at the time, wrote on the social platform X just after 6 p.m. that he heard “about 30 shots” before being ushered inside.

“Immediately the Secret Service came up from behind us screaming, ‘get out of here, get down, shots fired, get to the briefing room,’” Flanagan said in a report on NewsNation. “They put everybody in the briefing room. We left our bags, we left cameras.”

Trump had canceled a planned weekend trip to his golf club in Bedminster, N.J., amid [reported negotiations](https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/5892964-trump-iran-deal-strait-hormuz/) with Iran, and wrote in a Truth Social post at 4:30 p.m. that he was “in the Oval Office.”

Law enforcement officials told NewsNation that Trump was still in the Oval Office working with several aides when the incident occurred.

“I am closely monitoring the shooting incident near the White House and will update the public as we have more information,” Homeland Security Secretary [Markwayne Mullin wrote on X](https://x.com/SecMullinDHS/status/2058347903530184764). “I am thankful for the bravery and swift action of the @SecretService officers involved. Thankfully, no officers were harmed.”

White House Communications Director [Steven Cheung](https://thehill.com/people/steven-cheung/) also wrote on X on Saturday that the president “has been working hard at the White House since early this morning.”

Trump was the target of a foiled [assassination attempt](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/what-we-know-suspect-white-house-correspondents-dinner-trump/) last month at the White House Correspondents’ Association dinner.
