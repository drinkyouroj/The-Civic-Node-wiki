## Raw Transcript Notes (summarized)

### Case Background (from Destiny's commentary)
- Plaintiff: "Pixie" / Jane Doe, represented by Joan (Joan Schlump Peters) and Carlos Chimera, Burke Bettler LLP
- Defendant: Steven K. Bonnell II ("Destiny"), represented by Jake/Jay Chimera and Joel Sitchell
- Case number: 25CV 20757, U.S. District Court, District of Florida
- Charge: Violation of a federal statute related to non-consensual sharing of intimate images
- Destiny's core defense: The alleged transmission occurred BEFORE the effective date of the federal statute Pixie is suing under — i.e., the court lacks subject matter jurisdiction

### The Catfishing / "Rose" Revelation
- Pixie's lawsuit alleged Destiny shared sexually explicit material with a minor ("Rose")
- "Solo" (screen name: Solo Leveling, real name claimed to be "Ben Conway") was a UK-based adult male (approximately 21-22 years old in 2022) who:
  - Met Rose (a real young woman in Illinois) on an app designed for underage children
  - Created fake Twitter, Discord, and Cash App accounts using Rose's identity and photos
  - Catfished Destiny for 2-3 years using Rose's accounts
  - Sent Destiny explicit content and received content from Destiny
  - Threatened Rose's family when she didn't cooperate; threatened to kill himself and kidnap her
  - Leaked selected Destiny content online in November 2024, maximizing reputational damage
- Rose testified at deposition (Oct 23, 2025, Rockford, Illinois) that:
  - She never communicated with Destiny
  - She never messaged, called, or corresponded electronically with Destiny
  - She did not control the Discord, Twitter, or Cash App accounts Solo created
  - She did not know who Pixie was and had never seen Pixie's videos
  - Solo was in the UK — explaining the European date format timestamps on leaked material
  - Solo was a grown adult (21-22) at the time of events (April 2022)
  - Solo had threatened Rose with death, threatened to kidnap her, had her create explicit content under coercion

### Key Legal Developments (Dec 2025)
- Motion to dismiss: Destiny's team filed that court lacked subject matter jurisdiction because the alleged video transmission predated the law
- Discovery dispute: Destiny alleges Pixie's side deleted/obscured evidence; Pixie's team tried to extend discovery deadline from Dec 16, 2025 to Feb 16, 2026
- Rule 11 sanctions: Both sides filed Rule 11 motions against each other
- Pixie's team added a false footnote claiming Rose was a minor during exchanges — later retracted as false when Rose's deposition proved otherwise
- Joan Peters reportedly represented at least 4 witnesses Destiny attempted to depose — blocking multiple subpoenas
- "Abby" witness: Pixie submitted an affidavit (not a deposition) from a person named "Abby" the night before an evidentiary hearing, then stopped producing her entirely
- Destiny alleges Lauren de Laguna (separate individual) coordinated with Pixie to destroy/obscure evidence; plans bar complaint against de Laguna after case ends

### The November 2024 Leak Mechanism
- On November 29, 2024, a KiwiFarms user named "SoloTinyLeaks" posted 26 explicit video clips of Destiny
- Destiny's stated explanation: "A friend of mine's account from when I talked to three or four years ago ended up getting compromised" — referring to Solo's fake "Rose" accounts
- Secondary sources indicate: some content was sent directly by Destiny via Discord to the "Rose" account (Solo); a separate portion (reportedly 3 videos) was from a Google Drive link Destiny had shared
- The Google Drive link was not a "hack" in the conventional sense — Destiny had shared it with the person he believed was Rose. Solo, as the controller of that fake account, had access to it and leaked the content in November 2024
- This distinction matters legally: Pixie's complaint alleges Destiny "shared" the video with Rose (true — he sent it to Solo's fake account), but the *public* leak came from Solo, not from Destiny's own action
