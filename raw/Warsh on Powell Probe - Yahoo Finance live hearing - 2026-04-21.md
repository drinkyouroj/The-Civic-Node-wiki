# Summary of relevant exchanges (extracted via WebFetch)

Kevin Warsh did NOT directly address the DOJ criminal probe of Jerome Powell during his Senate Banking Committee confirmation hearing on April 21, 2026. When asked about a closely adjacent independence question — the firing of Fed Governor Lisa Cook — he deflected:

> "It's pending before the United States Supreme Court. It's inappropriate for me to weigh in on that." — Kevin Warsh

Senator Thom Tillis (R-NC) used his question time to attack the DOJ probe directly, calling it "bogus" and saying "Let's get rid of this investigation so I can support your confirmation." Tillis directed this at the administration / DOJ — not as a question to Warsh. Warsh did not affirmatively defend Powell or criticize the probe in response.

On general Trump pressure, Warsh said:
> "The president never once asked me to commit to any particular interest rate decision, period. Nor would I ever agree to do so if he had... I will be an independent actor if confirmed as chair of the Federal Reserve."

He also said he would not be Trump's "sock puppet."

## Pattern observed
Across the hearing record, Warsh:
- Affirmed abstract Fed independence
- Refused to defend Powell by name
- Used the "pending matter" dodge to avoid commenting on the Cook firing (a direct parallel to the DOJ probe)
- Did not volunteer any view on the criminal investigation
