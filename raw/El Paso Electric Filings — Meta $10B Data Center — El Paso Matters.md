El Paso Matters reporting on El Paso Electric utility filings with the Public Utility Commission of Texas detailing the infrastructure required to power Meta's expanded $10 billion data center in El Paso. Meta increased its El Paso investment from $1.5 billion to $10 billion in March 2026.

Power infrastructure planned:
- 366-megawatt facility built by El Paso Electric
- Cost: nearly $500 million
- 813 modular gas-fired generators from Enchanted Rock (Houston-based)

Cost-recovery structure:
- Meta pays full costs during a "bridge period" of 1-5 years
- After the bridge period, El Paso Electric incorporates costs into general customer rates
- Public Utility Commission of Texas approval required for the rate transition

Annual emissions profile:
- 117 metric tons of nitrogen oxides (a 5% increase to El Paso Electric's NOx emissions)
- 2,700 metric tons of carbon monoxide
- 42 metric tons of particulate matter

Water consumption: estimated 400,000 gallons daily for the data center; El Paso Water's total supply averages 110 million gallons daily.

Employment: Meta pledged 300 permanent operating jobs and 4,000+ construction workers; the tax agreement requires only 50 hires.

Community investments: $500,000 education grant program; $25,000 utility bill assistance; water/sewer service to 100 homes via DigDeep partnership.

Direct quotes:
- James Schichtl, El Paso Electric VP: "There will be no rate impact on any customer other than Meta during the bridge period."
- Chris Canales, El Paso City Council Rep: "I wouldn't support it if I were voting on this today."
- Matthew Rodriguez, Amanecer People's Project organizer: "This is completely irresponsible."
- Kelly Tomblin, El Paso Electric CEO: "Meta will add to our bigger vision for growing this region."
- Jon Barela, Borderplex Alliance CEO: "These jobs are worth it. They're high-paying and transformational."

Source/methodology: utility regulatory filings with the Public Utility Commission of Texas; company announcements; public meeting testimony; emissions data from Enchanted Rock technical specifications.
