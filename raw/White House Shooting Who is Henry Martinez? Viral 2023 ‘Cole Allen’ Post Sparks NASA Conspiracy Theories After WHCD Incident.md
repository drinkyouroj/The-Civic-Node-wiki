A fresh wave of online speculation has emerged following the White House Correspondents’ Dinner shooting, not just around the suspect but also a mysterious social media post that resurfaced days later.

As authorities continue their investigation, internet users have turned their attention to a little-known account linked to the name Henry Martinez. The post, made in 2023 and referencing “Cole Allen”, has gone viral after the suspect’s identity became public, triggering a series of theories online, many of which remain unverified.

## White House Shooting: Who is Henry Martinez?

Little is known about Henry Martinez, the individual whose name is now trending online. A social media account under this name reportedly shared a single post in 2023 that mentioned “Cole Allen”. The account has no other visible activity, which has added to the intrigue.

The lack of information about the account holder has made it difficult to verify whether Henry Martinez is a real individual, a pseudonym or an inactive user. As a result, authorities have not confirmed any connection between this account and the ongoing investigation.

## White House Shooting: 2023 ‘Cole Allen’ Post Goes Viral

The resurfaced post gained traction shortly after Cole Tomas Allen was identified in reports as the suspect in the White House Correspondents’ Dinner shooting. The timing led many online users to question whether the post had any predictive or hidden meaning.

Despite the viral attention, there is no verified evidence linking the post to the incident. Experts caution that such coincidences often fuel misinformation, especially during high-profile breaking news events.

## White House Shooting: Suspect’s NASA Link & Conspiracy Theories

The situation intensified when a 2014 technical publication authored by someone named Henry Martinez was discovered in connection with NASA. This led to speculation about a possible link between the social media account and the scientific author.

Adding to the narrative, Cole Tomas Allen reportedly participated in a summer research programme at NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory during his academic years. The overlap in names and timelines has encouraged online conspiracy theories.

However, there is no confirmed evidence that the Henry Martinez who authored the NASA document is the same person behind the viral social media account, or that either is connected to the suspect.

## White House Shooting: What Authorities Have Said?

Law enforcement agencies have not commented on the viral post or the speculation surrounding it. Officials have instead focused on the facts of the case and the suspect’s actions during the incident.

Authorities confirmed that the suspect, identified as Cole Tomas Allen, was taken into custody after attempting to breach security at the event. They have urged the public to avoid drawing conclusions based on unverified online claims.

## White House Shooting: Details of the WHCD Shooting

The incident took place during the White House Correspondents’ Dinner in Washington, where Donald Trump, JD Vance and Melania Trump were present.

Officials said the suspect was armed with multiple weapons, including a shotgun, a handgun and knives, when he attempted to enter a restricted area. Security personnel quickly intervened, preventing further escalation.

No fatalities were reported, and all senior officials were safely evacuated from the venue.

## White House Shooting: Who is Cole Tomas Allen?

Cole Tomas Allen, a 31-year-old from Torrance, California, has been identified in reports as the suspect. According to publicly available information, he has an academic background in engineering and computer science.

He is said to have graduated from the California Institute of Technology in 2017 and later earned a master’s degree from California State University Dominguez Hills in 2025. Authorities confirmed that he was taken into custody and is expected to face legal proceedings following the incident.

## White House Shooting: Investigation Continues
