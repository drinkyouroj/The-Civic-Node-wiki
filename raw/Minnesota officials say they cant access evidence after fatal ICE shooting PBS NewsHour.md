Published January 8, 2026, 11:41 AM EDT (updated 6:57 PM EDT). Byline: Associated Press, via PBS NewsHour.

Minnesota's Public Safety Commissioner Bob Jacobson said the state cannot effectively prosecute the ICE agent who shot Renée Good without federal cooperation.

Key verbatim quote from Jacobson:

> "For us to be able to do that, it would be extremely difficult, if not impossible, without cooperation from the federal government."

Jacobson's context: state agents normally work with the FBI on such investigations — the FBI holds "the evidence in the original investigative notes and reports." In this case: "We have none of that." The FBI reversed course after an initial cooperation agreement with the Minnesota BCA, the U.S. Attorney's office in Minneapolis, and the Hennepin County Attorney's office, and has blocked the BCA from case materials.
