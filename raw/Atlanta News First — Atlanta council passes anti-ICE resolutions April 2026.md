# Atlanta council passes resolutions opposing ICE detention facilities, setting police guidelines

**Author:** Alani Letang | **Published:** April 20, 2026 | **Source:** Atlanta News First

## Vote Date

**April 20, 2026** (Monday). Article URL contains /04/21/ but article was published April 20.

## The Two Resolutions

1. **Anti-detention resolution:** Opposition to large immigration detention facilities within Atlanta
2. **APD guidelines resolution:** Requirements for the Atlanta Police Department to document federal immigration activity, including preserving body camera footage during ICE interactions
