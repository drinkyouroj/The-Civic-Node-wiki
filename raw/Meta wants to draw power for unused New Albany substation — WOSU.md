WOSU 89.7 NPR News reporting on AEP Ohio and Meta's joint Public Utilities Commission of Ohio (PUCO) filing (filed Monday, Nov 24, 2025) requesting approval for Meta's data center to draw power from the Green Chapel substation under a three-year temporary arrangement.

Background: The Green Chapel substation was constructed to serve Intel's $28 billion chip fabrication project in central Ohio. The substation was completed earlier in 2025 but remained unused after Intel's July 2025 announcement to slow construction. Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan said in July 2025 the company "invested too much, too soon — without adequate demand." Intel's plant production start dates were originally slated for 2030 and 2031.

Filing terms:
- Service window: January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2028
- AEP Ohio would construct four temporary power lines connecting Green Chapel to Meta's nearby New Albany data center
- Meta's electricity charges will cover the full three-year cost of the Green Chapel substation, offsetting AEP Ohio's financial losses from Intel's revised timeline
- Cost-recovery framing per AEP Ohio: absent Meta, "the cost of Green Chapel substation would be recovered through rates paid by all AEP customers"

Meta's New Albany footprint: $1.5 billion in capital investments and 300 full-time jobs since the 2017 announcement.

Direct quotes:
- Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan (July 2025): the company "invested too much, too soon — without adequate demand."
- Intel spokesperson on the Meta arrangement: "Proposal provides productive solution benefiting local community."

Source/methodology: WOSU public-media reporting based on the Nov 24, 2025 PUCO filing; statements from AEP Ohio and Intel representatives.
