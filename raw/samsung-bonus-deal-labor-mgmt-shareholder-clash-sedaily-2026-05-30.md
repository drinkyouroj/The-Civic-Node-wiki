**Deal terms cited:**
- Special management performance bonus funded by 10.5% of Device Solutions (DS / chip) division operating profit.
- Overall Performance Incentive (OPI) system based on 10% of operating profit.

**Shareholders' core argument:** the bonus agreement "bypasses the dividend procedures stipulated under the Commercial Act" by distributing profits before corporate-tax deductions and before shareholder approval.
