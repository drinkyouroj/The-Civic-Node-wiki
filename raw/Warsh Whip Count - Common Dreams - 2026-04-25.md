Senate Banking Republicans scheduled a late-night vote for April 29 to advance Kevin Warsh, Trump's Fed chair nominee, shortly after DOJ dropped its Powell probe.

## Warren's framing
Sen. Elizabeth Warren (D-MA): "either the Republican majority is fooled easily or they are hoping to fool the American people." She argues DOJ ceased the Powell investigation only to enable Warsh's confirmation while preserving the threat of restarting it.

Warren on Warsh: "proved in his nomination hearing to be nothing more than President Trump's sock puppet."

## Hearing concerns Democrats raised
- Financial transparency: Warsh disclosed $135-226M in assets but refused to detail >$100M in holdings, citing confidentiality agreements.
- Independence: Warsh declined during the hearing to confirm that Trump lost the 2020 election.
- FBI background: Warren reported FBI conducted no investigation into Warsh's undisclosed financial holdings or his appearance in Epstein-related files.

## Timeline
Vote positions Warsh for full Senate confirmation before Powell's term ends May 15.
