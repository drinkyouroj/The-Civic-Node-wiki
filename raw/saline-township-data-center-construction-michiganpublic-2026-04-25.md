# Saline Township data center: "major construction" underway, financing secured

*Michigan Public — April 25, 2026 — Brett Dahlberg*

## Project details
- **Developers/owners:** Related Digital and Blackstone are building the facility.
- **Tenant:** Oracle is the technology company for which it's being constructed.
- **Power supply:** DTE Energy.
- **Location:** Saline Township (Washtenaw County, outside Ann Arbor).
- **Investment:** $16 billion project.
- **Power capacity:** will draw "more than a gigawatt of electricity — about 25%" of DTE's current peak capacity.

## Stated benefits (per company statement)
- 2,500 union construction jobs
- 450 on-site jobs
- 1,500 county-wide jobs
- $14 million in direct community benefits
- 750 acres of preserved open space, farmland, and wetlands

Related Digital and Blackstone: "Major construction is well underway in Saline Township. Momentum is strong."

## Controversy
The project remains contentious over environmental impacts and energy demands. Michigan Attorney General **Dana Nessel** has filed an appeal of the MPSC's **conditional approval of DTE's power supply contracts**, citing insufficient transparency and redacted contract details.

*(Context from related coverage: the campus is part of the OpenAI/Oracle "Stargate" program and is nicknamed "The Barn" for a historic red barn preserved at the site entrance along Michigan Avenue; construction began Q1 2026, targeting completion Q4 2027.)*
