# GBPI's statement on the signing of House Bill 1105

**Author:** David Schaefer, VP of Research and Policy at GBPI | **Published:** May 1, 2024

## Key Content

Governor Brian Kemp signed HB 1105 into law on Wednesday (April 2024 / signing reported May 1, 2024). The legislation requires sheriffs in Georgia to work with federal immigration authorities when someone in custody is believed to be undocumented.

## What It Requires

Sheriffs must coordinate with Immigration and Customs Enforcement regarding individuals suspected of being in the country without authorization. Non-compliance carries penalties including potential loss of state funding.

## GBPI's Concerns

David Schaefer stated the measure "effectively turns the entire state into a 287(g) jurisdiction, mandating deeper local cooperation with Immigration and Customs Enforcement." He emphasized the law provides no additional funding despite creating new obligations, strains local resources, and may expand detention of people of color while separating immigrant families.
