# At Hearing, Warren Delivers Remarks on Kevin Warsh's Nomination for Fed Chair

Summary of Sen. Elizabeth Warren's opening statement as ranking member of the Senate Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs Committee at the April 21, 2026 confirmation hearing for Kevin Warsh as Federal Reserve Chair.

## Opening context

Warren stated that the hearing should not be occurring due to Trump's "economic failures" — record-low consumer sentiment, rising inflation, and increased costs for families.

## Central criticism of Trump's Fed interference

> "The President has repeatedly and illegally attempted to take over the Fed"

— through attacks on officials designed to threaten members into compliance, according to Warren's remarks.

## Assessment of Warsh's record

Warren characterized Warsh as

> "uniquely ill-suited for the job"

noting his role during the 2008 financial crisis when he dismissed housing concerns and later arranged "multibillion-dollar bailouts" for Wall Street while ordinary Americans suffered. Per other reporting, Warren noted Warsh served as a Fed governor from 2006 to 2011, was "an enthusiastic cheerleader for credit default swaps and complex securitizations" before the crash, dismissed concerns from housing advocates about subprime mortgages, and refused to use the Fed's authorities to address building financial system risks.

## Pattern of position-shifting

Warren documented Warsh's changing stance on interest rates, describing how he shifted positions based on political circumstances, most recently aligning with Trump's preferred policies once Trump returned to office.

## On Trump's motivation

> "Trump's economic failures are causing him political problems and he wants the Fed to use monetary policy to artificially juice the economy in the short term and this is his last chance to do that before the November election."

## Closing position

Warren called for postponing the hearing and conducting oversight into Trump's direction of investigations into Fed officials, stating the Senate

> "should not be aiding and abetting Donald Trump's illegal takeover of the Fed."
