Cantor Equity Partners 1, a blank-check investment vehicle led by Brandon Lutnick (son of U.S. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick), is in advanced negotiations to establish a substantial Bitcoin treasury. The Financial Times first reported the discussions on July 15, 2025, citing two unnamed sources familiar with the matter.

The proposed transaction involves receiving approximately 30,000 BTC from Blockstream Capital founder Adam Back. Additionally, the SPAC plans to raise up to $800 million in external capital for further Bitcoin acquisitions, bringing the total deal valuation above $4 billion.

Cantor Equity Partners 1 completed a $200 million initial public offering at the start of 2025. This deal follows Cantor Fitzgerald's April 2025 announcement of "Twenty One," another SPAC-backed Bitcoin treasury with a projected $3.9 billion war chest, backed by Tether, Bitfinex, and SoftBank.

Neither Cantor Equity Partners 1 nor Blockstream Capital responded to Decrypt's request for comment at time of publication.

The discussions occurred amid increased activity in crypto-focused SPAC transactions, supported by the Trump administration's favorable regulatory stance. Multiple Bitcoin treasury companies have recently announced public offerings through similar vehicles.

The combined company is expected to trade on Nasdaq under the ticker "BSTR" (Bitcoin Standard Treasury).
