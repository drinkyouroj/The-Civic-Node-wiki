# AI Data Center Demand Drove 76 Percent Surge in Wholesale Power Prices Across East Coast Grid

## Price surge

Wholesale electricity prices across PJM Interconnection's 13-state grid jumped sharply in Q1 2026.

- Q1 2025: $77.78/MWh
- Q1 2026: $136.53/MWh
- Increase: 76% year-over-year

## Capacity cost surge

- Capacity costs alone: +398% Q1 2026 YoY
- Transmission costs: ~5% increase (comparison)
- Recent capacity auctions added ~$13 billion to customer bills across the 13-state grid
- Costs ultimately borne by residential and commercial ratepayers, not technology companies

## Geographic pressure

Northern Virginia — the world's densest concentration of data centers — emerged as the primary strain point on system reliability.

## Regulatory response

Monitoring Analytics (PJM's independent market monitor) characterized the impacts as "significant and irreversible" and recommended:
- Requiring data centers to bring their own generation capacity
- Creating dedicated interconnection queues for data centers contingent on available generation
- Implementing longer-term capacity commitments

PJM responses:
- Capped future capacity prices through 2029
- Released reform proposals

Political response:
- Multiple state officials (including Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro) have threatened withdrawal from PJM if pricing continues climbing
