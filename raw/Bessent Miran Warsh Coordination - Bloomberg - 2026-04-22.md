# Warsh Wants Fed to Back Bessent-Rubio 'Statecraft' Agenda for the Dollar

NOTE: Bloomberg article body could not be retrieved directly (403). Captured via search-result excerpt and downstream coverage.

## Headline framing
The Bloomberg newsletter (April 22, 2026) explicitly frames Kevin Warsh — Trump's Fed Chair nominee — as wanting the Federal Reserve to back a Treasury/State Department "statecraft" agenda for the dollar, with Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Secretary of State Marco Rubio named as the architects. This is the most direct mainstream-press characterization to date placing Warsh inside the administration's economic-policy orbit rather than independent of it.

## Key takeaways from search excerpts and downstream coverage
- Warsh, in his confirmation testimony (April 21, 2026) and prior writings, called for a new Treasury-Fed accord coordinating balance-sheet runoff with Treasury debt issuance.
- Warsh described the Fed as "independent inside of government, not independent of government."
- Warsh called the Fed's large balance sheet "fiscal policy in disguise" and said he "explicitly wants to work with the Treasury Secretary to coordinate a reduction."
- The Bloomberg framing connects Warsh's monetary stance to a broader administration "managed dollar decline" agenda being run out of Treasury (Bessent) and State (Rubio).

## Why this matters for the coordination claim
This is the first piece in mainstream financial press to assert — in its headline — that Warsh's Fed agenda is downstream of a Bessent-led economic strategy. It is editorial framing rather than reported direct evidence (no leaked memos, no readouts of meetings), but it indicates that Bloomberg's economics desk now treats the alignment as the operating assumption.
