Lauren DeLaguna (legal name Lauren Hayden; sometimes referred to by DGG as "Lauren Northern") is a former orbiter that would frequently engage with Destiny on conservative political ideology and law.

**Personal Information**
- Status: Active
- Political Alignment: Conservative
- Qualifications: Lawyer
- Occupation: Content Creator
- Country of Origin: United States

**Online Information**
- X: laurendelaguna
- Instagram: laurendelaguna
- YouTube: @LaurenDeLaguna

She had several disagreements with Pisco, a fellow lawyer, regarding the January 6 insurrection, Donald Trump's indictments, and other political flashpoints. She has had numerous appearances on the Fresh&Fit men's self-improvement and red pill podcast. She also briefly dated fan of the stream Martin Shkreli, who was imprisoned for two counts of securities fraud and one count of conspiracy to commit securities fraud. She and Martin Shkreli have appeared together on Destiny's streams.

Lauren was notable in that she appeared on IRL streams with Destiny on a few occasions. Her last appearance in the DGG Cinematic Universe was on May 12, 2023 along with Shkreli.

Lauren and Destiny had a surprise encounter on Nov 13, 2025, in Austin, Texas. Destiny later talked about it on stream.

**Blacklist**: Lauren is currently on Destiny's Blacklist until March 6th, 2025.
