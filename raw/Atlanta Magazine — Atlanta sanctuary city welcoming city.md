# Does it matter if Atlanta is declared a Sanctuary City versus a Welcoming City?

**Author:** Scott Henry | **Published:** February 9, 2017 | **Source:** Atlanta Magazine

## Policy Distinction

Mayor Kasim Reed rejected the "sanctuary city" designation in favor of "welcoming city," asserting: "The priority is keeping the people of Atlanta safe and not executing federal mandates related to immigration."

## Legal Context

Georgia law passed in 2009 makes sanctuary cities illegal in the state. President Trump threatened to strip federal funding from sanctuary jurisdictions, creating financial and legal risks for any city adopting that status.

## Definition Differences

A sanctuary city unofficially refuses cooperation with federal immigration enforcement in identifying or deporting undocumented immigrants. No official legal definition exists. Examples include New Orleans (prohibiting information-sharing with ICE) and Boston (refusing to detain undocumented immigrants pending deportation).

## Atlanta's Approach

Rather than declaring sanctuary status, Atlanta created the Mayor's Office of Immigrant Affairs in late 2014 and launched the Welcoming Atlanta initiative, designed to support newcomers through improved municipal outreach while making no explicit mention of ICE cooperation.

## Quotes
