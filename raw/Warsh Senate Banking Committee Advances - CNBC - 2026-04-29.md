Senate Banking Committee voted 13-11 along strict party lines to advance Kevin Warsh's nomination for Federal Reserve Chair. First fully partisan committee vote on a Fed chair nominee in the committee's history. All Republicans yes; all Democrats no.

- Full Senate floor vote expected week of May 11, 2026
- Powell's term expires May 15, 2026; Powell agreed to remain until Warsh confirmed
- Warsh needs simple majority; Republicans hold 53 seats
- Sen. Thom Tillis (R-NC) had blocked Warsh over DOJ Powell probe; dropped hold after probe dropped
- Sen. John Fetterman (D-PA) told Semafor he plans to vote for Warsh — potential bipartisan cover
- Warsh: former Fed governor; current Hoover Institution fellow at Stanford
- Democrats argued confirming Warsh would compromise Fed independence

Quotes:
- Kevin Warsh: "Monetary policy independence is essential. Monetary policymakers must act in [the nation's interest]."
- Kevin Warsh: "Central bankers must be strong enough to listen to a diversity of views from all [corners]."
- Sen. Elizabeth Warren (D-MA): "You said it's perfectly fine for elected officials to state their views on interest rates. But that's not what Donald Trump is [doing]."
- Sen. Tim Scott (R-SC): "An independent Federal Reserve is essential to achieving its mission. That independence must be [protected]."
