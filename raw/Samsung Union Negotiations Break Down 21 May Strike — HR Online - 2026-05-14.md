# Negotiations break down: Samsung Electronics union to stage strike as planned on 21 May 2026

## Union demands

- Bonus allocation: 15% of operating profit directed to performance bonuses
- Bonus cap removal: elimination of current bonus cap restrictions
- Contract formalization: inclusion of bonus provisions in employment contracts

## Management counter-proposal

- Bonus allocation: 10% of operating profit as bonuses
- Additional compensation: one-time special package "exceeding industry standards"
- Position: management stated the union's demands would be "unsustainable in the long term"

## Negotiation collapse

The union and management held two mediation rounds on 11–12 May 2026 under South Korea's Ministry of Employment and Labor. The final session lasted 17 hours but failed to produce agreement.

Union statement (paraphrased by Yonhap): "Management had not meaningfully responded to its demands, and no further talks are being considered ahead of the planned strike."

## Strike details

- Planned start: 21 May 2026
- Duration: 18 days
- Expected participation: 41,000 members indicated willingness; estimates suggest participation could exceed 50,000 workers
- Estimated economic impact: potential losses up to 40 trillion won
