In the immediate aftermath of the attack on the [White House Correspondents’ Dinner](https://www.wired.com/story/california-engineer-identified-in-suspected-shooting-at-white-house-correspondents-dinner/) on Saturday night, influencers, pundits, and random posters lit up social media platforms like [X](https://x.com/search?q=staged&src=typed_query), [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/search?q=staged), and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DXl8GJjEdPF/) with [conspiracy theories](https://www.wired.com/story/maga-is-increasingly-convinced-the-trump-assassination-attempt-was-staged/) about the attack and the alleged shooter.

Both left- and right-wing accounts claimed, without evidence, that the attack was staged.

President Donald Trump, Vice President JD Vance, and dozens of other high-profile administration officials and journalists were attending the dinner at the Hilton hotel in Washington, DC, when a suspect, later [identified](https://apnews.com/article/white-house-correspondents-dinner-trump-first-amendment-a0a2446832e8596e66c6fccb8426c8aa) by media reports as Cole Tomas Allen from California, allegedly ran past security towards the event. He was detained by law enforcement while the president and vice president were evacuated. Police [said](https://apnews.com/article/white-house-correspondents-dinner-trump-first-amendment-a0a2446832e8596e66c6fccb8426c8aa) they believe Cole acted alone, but did not expand on who his intended target was or what his motive may have been. “We believe the suspect was targeting administration officials,” acting attorney general Todd Blanche [told](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/correspondents-dinner-suspected-shooter-trump-administration-officials-rcna342158) NBC’s *Meet the Press* on Sunday morning.

On Bluesky, which has a predominantly left-leaning user base, many people simply wrote the word “ [STAGED](https://bsky.app/search?q=staged) ” over and over again, echoing the response to the [Trump assassination attempt](https://www.wired.com/story/thomas-crooks-conspiracy-donald-trump-cia-mk-ultra/) in Butler, Pennsylvania, in 2024.

On X, many [claimed](https://x.com/acnewsitics/status/2048248959597228373) [the shooting](https://x.com/Acquired_Savant/status/2048236450420867160) was [staged](https://x.com/aliasvaughn/status/2048346748922351788) as a way to bolster support for Trump’s plan to build a new ballroom in the White House. The president referenced the ballroom in a press conference after the incident and a Truth Social [post](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/116471074928310119) on Sunday morning. [Many prominent online Trump boosters](https://x.com/Acquired_Savant/status/2048278298996650041/photo/1) echoed the need for the ballroom, including far-right podcaster [Jack Posobiec](https://x.com/JackPosobiec/status/2048223893106675734), Libs of TikTok creator [Chaya Raichik](https://x.com/libsoftiktok/status/2048223486028497115), and [Tom Fitton](https://x.com/TomFitton/status/2048224348599685224), the right-wing activist who runs Judicial Watch.

Their quick response, conspiracy theorists claimed, was evidence of a coordinated campaign following the shooting. “Is this another staged event,” one X user asked in a post that has been [viewed more than 5 million times](https://x.com/Acquired_Savant/status/2048236450420867160).

Other social media users who claimed the incident was staged [pointed](https://x.com/infolibnews/status/2048222643237601457) to a Fox News clip that featured the station’s White House correspondent Aishah Hasnie speaking from the Hilton hotel. Hasnie told viewers that prior to the shooting, press secretary Karoline Leavitt’s husband allegedly told her, “You need to be very safe,” before the call was cut off.

“Fox News just cut one of their reporters off as they seemed to indicate the shooting was a preplanned false flag,” one X user [wrote](https://x.com/micah_erfan/status/2048242233313214864) in a post that has been viewed more than 2 million times. Hasnie later clarified in [an X post](https://x.com/aishahhasnie/status/2048274579043336397) that her cell service had cut out in a location with notoriously bad service, adding: “He was telling me to be careful with my own safety because the world is crazy. He was expressing his concern for my safety.”

“I don't want to be fomenting conspiracies,” wrote [Angelo Carusone](https://bsky.app/profile/goangelo.bsky.social/post/3mkeibhtom22g), the chair and president of Media Matters, on Bluesky about the Fox News interview. “But I mean … this was super weird. Super weird.”

Leavitt herself was also the focus of conspiracy theories after she said “ [shots will be fired](https://ew.com/there-will-be-some-shots-fired-karoline-leavitt-joked-before-gunfire-at-correspondents-dinner-11958857) ” in an interview ahead of the dinner, referring to the jokes Trump was scheduled to deliver. Following the attack, X users claimed the comment was “ [strange](https://x.com/infolibnews/status/2048225457330434481),” “ [sus](https://x.com/Sjacobs2020/status/2048214357436469443),” or a “ [curious choice of words](https://x.com/rabbitSZN2/status/2048223367548080203),” while sharing memes that suggested the shooting was staged. At least one [mainstream outlet](https://www.thedailybeast.com/karoline-leavitt-made-bizarre-shots-fired-brag-minutes-before-shooting/) appeared to amplify the conspiracy theory as well, describing Leavitt's comment as “eerie” and “bizarre.”

“A lot of people are saying they think the WHCD shooting was staged, as a way to change the narrative from his abysmal approval ratings and his bumbling of the Iran War,” Majid Padellan, a progressive social media influencer known online as Brooklyn Dad, [posted](https://x.com/mmpadellan/status/2048224028536521032) on X to his 1.3 million followers. “What do YOU think? Staged or not staged?” A debate ensued in his comment section, with many followers claiming it was indeed staged.

There were also some right-wing figures raising the possibility that the attack was staged. “Was this staged,” Sam Parker, a self-described America First nationalist with a large online following [wrote on X](https://x.com/BasedSamParker/status/2048242053805740506), referencing Leavitt’s comments and footage of Erika Kirk leaving the event in tears.

These conspiracy theories have continued to spread without evidence. Nothing new has been uncovered about the alleged shooter’s motivation, and conspiracy theories around incidents tend to evolve rapidly in the days and weeks following the event. Opinions are still being formed: Far-right conspiracy theorist Alex Jones, for example, went from [questioning](https://x.com/RealAlexJones/status/2048353261065756737) whether Saturday night’s incident was staged to [saying it wasn’t](https://x.com/RealAlexJones/status/2048379683234853362) in the space of a couple of hours.

Former congresswoman Marjorie Taylor Greene, another noted conspiracy theorist, hasn’t appeared to have made up her mind. “Many questions about Cole Allen,” she [wrote on X](https://x.com/FmrRepMTG/status/2048384732224749678) on Saturday night. “People researched quickly and found some interesting things.”
