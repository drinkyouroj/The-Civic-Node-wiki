# Michigan environmental news: DTE ties future rate freeze to data centers

*Planet Detroit — April 24, 2026 — Dustin Blitchok, Managing Editor*

DTE Energy is proposing a **$474.3 million** electric rate increase — about **7.6%**, adding roughly **$9.39 per month** to the average residential bill — with implementation targeted for **early 2027**.

DTE promised "to pause further rate increase requests for at least two years, citing revenue expected from large **Oracle and Google data centers** being built in its service territory."

The **Michigan Public Service Commission (MPSC)** is expected to rule on the request "within 10 months."

The item references Crain's Detroit Business as the underlying reporting.
