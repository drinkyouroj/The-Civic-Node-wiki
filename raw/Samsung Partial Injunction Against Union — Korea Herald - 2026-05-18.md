# Samsung wins partial injunction against unions ahead of strike

The Suwon District Court partially approved Samsung Electronics' request for an injunction against labor actions on May 18, 2026.

## Injunction specifics

The court mandated that union members maintain normal staffing at:
- Safety and security-related facilities
- Equipment preservation operations
- Semiconductor wafer contamination prevention work

The injunction also barred the union and leader Choi Seung-ho from:
- Occupying facilities
- Installing locks
- Obstructing worker entry

## Legal framing

The ruling "largely accepted management's argument that disruptions to critical facilities could threaten safety and semiconductor production."

## Context

Approximately 50,000 workers planned an 18-day strike from May 21–June 7 over bonus disputes. The union demanded:
- Removal of a 50% annual salary performance bonus cap
- 15% of operating profit allocation as bonuses

Samsung opposed permanently removing the ceiling but offered industry-leading performance rewards.

(Korea Herald reports ~50,000 workers, vs. Fortune and HR Online figures of 45,000 and 41,000+. Variance is from union polling vs. official tallies.)
