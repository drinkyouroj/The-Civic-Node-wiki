## Overview

"Pixie" (Jane Doe) is an online content creator and the plaintiff in a federal civil lawsuit against [[Destiny]] (Steven K. Bonnell II), filed in 2025. She is pursuing a claim under a federal non-consensual intimate image statute. Her real identity is not publicly confirmed; she is suing pseudonymously. She is reportedly pursuing a law degree in Illinois at the time of the lawsuit.
