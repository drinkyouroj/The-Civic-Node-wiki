## Overview
Active-duty U.S. Army Master Sergeant stationed at [[Fort Bragg]]; member of the special-forces team that planned and executed [[Operation Absolute Resolve]], the January 2026 covert capture of [[Nicolás Maduro]]. Indicted in the Southern District of New York in April 2026 for allegedly betting on the operation's success on [[Polymarket]] using classified insider knowledge — first high-profile criminal prosecution of [[Insider Trading on Prediction Markets]] tied to a national-security operation.

## Key Facts
- Master Sergeant — senior NCO, key tactical leader at Army battalion level — per [[Special Forces Soldier Polymarket Insider Trading — CNN]]
- Stationed at [[Fort Bragg]]
- Opened [[Polymarket]] account late December 2025; placed 13 bets between December 27 and January 2; final bet placed hours before the overnight Maduro capture
- Initial wager: ~$32,000 on Maduro being "out" by January (a long-shot at the time)
- Profit: $400,000+
- Routed winnings through a foreign cryptocurrency vault → online brokerage account (alleged concealment)
- 5 SDNY criminal charges: stealing/misusing confidential government information, theft, fraud — Jay Clayton, US Attorney
- Parallel CFTC civil complaint
- Photographed post-operation on a ship at sea at sunrise in U.S. military fatigues

## Newsletter Relevance
The first criminal defendant in a prediction-market insider-trading case tied to U.S. national-security operations. His prosecution sets the playbook (DOJ + CFTC parallel filings; SDNY venue; foreign crypto vault detection) for any future cases.
