## Overview
President of [[OpenAI]]; co-founder (with [[Sam Altman]] and [[Elon Musk]] in 2015). Co-defendant alongside [[Sam Altman]] and [[OpenAI]] in *Musk v. Altman*; on the witness list for the trial beginning April 28, 2026 in Oakland.

## Key Facts
- Co-founded [[OpenAI]] (2015) with [[Sam Altman]] and [[Elon Musk]]
- Co-defendant in *Musk v. Altman* — Musk's $134B suit alleging breach of charitable trust over OpenAI's for-profit conversion — see [[Musk v Altman Trial Preview — CNBC]]
- Musk seeks Brockman's removal from OpenAI leadership as part of the requested remedies
- Witness list for trial includes Brockman alongside Musk, Altman, and [[Satya Nadella]]

Stub. Brockman's role in OpenAI's governance — and whether Musk's removal demand is granted in any form — is one of the load-bearing questions in the [[AI Antitrust]] cluster.
