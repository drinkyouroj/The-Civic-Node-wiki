## Overview

Crusoe Energy is a US energy infrastructure company focused on building "AI factory" data centers with on-site power generation. Active operator at the Stargate Abilene, Texas campus — has built two AI factory buildings and an on-site power plant on the 1,000-acre site. Crusoe's on-site generation pattern is the operational template for hyperscaler vertical integration as workaround to [[Interconnection Queue]] depth. Andrew Likens serves as the company's energy lead.

## Key Facts

- **Stargate Abilene operations:** Operating two new "AI factory" buildings at the Abilene site after the OpenAI/Oracle expansion cancellation in March 2026; building an on-site power plant. [[Stargate Data Center Expansion Cancelled — Oracle and OpenAI]]
- **Andrew Likens (energy lead) on supply-chain bottlenecks:** "If one piece of your supply chain is delayed, then your whole project can't deliver." [[Almost Half of US Data Centers That Were Supposed to Open This Year Slated to Be Canceled or Delayed — Futurism - 2026-04-02]]
- **Meta capacity discussions:** Reportedly in talks with [[Meta]] (with [[NVIDIA]]'s involvement) regarding excess Abilene capacity post-OpenAI/Oracle cancellation.
