## Overview

Lauren de Laguna is a Florida attorney who [[Destiny]] alleges has been a behind-the-scenes coordinator in [[Pixie]]'s lawsuit against him, despite not being Pixie's official counsel of record. Destiny accuses her of contacting at least 9 content creators and journalists to generate negative coverage of him during litigation, coordinating with Pixie to destroy discoverable evidence, and reaching out to major figures including Hasan Piker and Ethan Klein to get them to amplify the case against Destiny.

## Key Facts

- **Legal name:** Lauren Hayden; online name "Lauren DeLaguna"; referred to as "Lauren Northern" within the Destiny community.
- **Background:** Conservative attorney and content creator. Qualifications: lawyer. Platforms: X (@laurendelaguna), Instagram, YouTube. Has appeared on Fresh&Fit podcast; briefly dated Martin Shkreli.
- **Last confirmed DGG appearance:** May 12, 2023 (with Shkreli). Was on Destiny's "Blacklist" until March 6, 2025 — during which time the lawsuit was filed (February 18, 2025).
- **November 13, 2025 encounter:** Had a surprise in-person encounter with Destiny in Austin, Texas, documented in the fan wiki; Destiny discussed it on stream. Occurred ~one month before her deposition.
- **Role:** Florida attorney; not Pixie's counsel of record, but a subpoenaed witness who has publicly admitted a facilitative role.
- **Admitted in her own X posts (Nov 20 and Dec 5, 2025):**
  - She is a subpoenaed witness and was deposed December 17, 2025.
  - She "spoke about the case to my friends."
  - She "asked a friend to represent Pxie."
  - She denies encouraging Pxie to file suit or giving Pxie legal advice.
- **Alleged activities (per Destiny — unconfirmed in public record):**
  - Contacted "10+ other creators behind the scenes to influence and attempt to direct their commentary on the case" (Destiny's Dec 6, 2025 X reply).
  - Gave guidance to content creator Amy Dangerfield on a video about the case.
  - Emailed Hasan Piker and Ethan Klein to get them to cover the case against Destiny.
  - Collaborated with Pixie to destroy/obscure discoverable evidence.
  - Was present when Rose ran up to Destiny and Lauren in person on film; Rose reportedly admitted in deposition she was FaceTiming with Pixie at the time.
  - No named creators have publicly confirmed being contacted by DeLaguna as of April 2026; no leaked DMs have surfaced.
