## Overview

"Solo" (screen name: Solo Leveling; claimed real name: Ben Conway; also known as Elero5 on Snapchat) is a UK-based adult male who catfished [[Destiny]] for approximately 2-3 years using the stolen identity of a young woman in Illinois ("[[Rose]]"). He created fake accounts across Discord, Twitter, and Cash App using Rose's photos, and engaged in a relationship with Destiny while posing as Rose. He is believed to be approximately 21-22 years old as of April 2022, and believed to be located in or near Oxford, UK.

## Key Facts

- Screen names: Solo Leveling, Elero5 (Snapchat), George Scorer (Cash App alias)
- Location: UK (believed Oxford area)
- Age in 2022: Approximately 21-22 (grown adult)
- Met Rose: On an app explicitly designed for underage children; November 2021
- Method: Created and operated fake Twitter ("Rose Coming"), Discord, and Cash App accounts using Rose's photos and identity
- Coercion of Rose: Threatened her with death, threatened to kidnap her and use her as a "sex slave" to coerce her into creating explicit content; then distributed that content to Destiny and others without Rose's consent
- Leaked content: On November 29, 2024, under the KiwiFarms handle "SoloTinyLeaks," posted 26 explicit video clips of Destiny publicly. Secondary sources indicate the leaked content included both videos Destiny had sent directly via Discord to Solo's fake "Rose" account AND content from a Google Drive link Destiny had shared with that account. The distinction: Destiny had shared this content believing he was sending it to Rose; Solo, who controlled the fake account, retained access to both the direct Discord messages and any shared storage links.
- Leak staging: Solo posted on KiwiFarms asking "What exactly can I leak here?" before posting "It's about to be a very white Christmas"
- UK timestamps: The European date format on leaked material was evidence of his UK location
- Bisexual per Rose's account; may have derived sexual gratification from the catfishing arrangement
- Described by Destiny as a "prolific catfisher" who likely targeted other women similarly

## Why He Matters

Solo is the person who actually caused the harm Pixie's lawsuit alleges against Destiny. Both Destiny and, reportedly, the first two attorneys Pixie consulted agreed: if there is a viable case, it is against Solo, not Destiny. Solo:
1. Stole and weaponized Rose's identity
2. Coerced Rose into creating explicit content under threats
3. Leaked Destiny's content publicly to cause maximum reputational damage
4. Created the entire fictitious "relationship" Destiny believed was with Rose

The fact that Solo is in the UK and not named as a party to the current lawsuit is a central strategic and legal puzzle in this case.

## Newsletter Relevance

Solo represents a category of harm the legal system has not caught up to: a third-party bad actor who uses identity theft to simultaneously victimize both the person whose identity is stolen AND the person being deceived, then uses the resulting mess as a weapon. Jurisdictional barriers (UK) make accountability difficult. His role also illustrates the gap between where damages actually originate and where litigation targets.
