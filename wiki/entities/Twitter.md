## Overview
Social network rebranded as X under Elon Musk's ownership. Central node in echo-chamber and political-polarization research.

## Key Facts
- Also referred to as "X" after the 2023 rebrand under Elon Musk
- 2025 algorithm (per marketing-trade descriptions): real-time topic-driven ranking, interest-based personalization, engagement-weighted scoring (likes, retweets, replies)
- Hosts "Spaces" (live audio) and creator Subscriptions as monetization layers

## Newsletter Relevance
- Primary venue for political speech in the US; the ranking system directly shapes civic discourse
- Real-time algorithm amplifies breaking news and trending events faster than other platforms, with attendant misinformation risk
