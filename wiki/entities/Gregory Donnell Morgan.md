## Overview
Gregory Donnell Morgan is an ICE agent charged on April 16, 2026 by Hennepin County with two felony counts of second-degree assault for allegedly pointing his gun at two drivers while operating an unmarked SUV on February 5, 2026, during [[Operation Metro Surge]]. He is reportedly the first federal immigration officer criminally charged under Trump's second-term immigration campaign in the Twin Cities, and per Hennepin County Attorney [[Mary Moriarty]], "the first case of its kind across the country."

## Key Facts
- Charged Apr. 16, 2026 by Hennepin County; two felony counts of second-degree assault [[Minnesota Prosecutors Charge an ICE Agent With Assault]]
- Incident date: Feb. 5, 2026; unmarked SUV; two civilian drivers as victims
- Maximum sentence: 36 months in prison
- Investigation triggered by victims' 911 calls — not federal evidence disclosure
- Participated in Operation Metro Surge

## Newsletter Relevance
Morgan's case is the first proof-of-concept for state prosecution of federal immigration agents during this operation. Because the evidence came from civilian 911 calls rather than federal evidence that the government has been withholding, the case reveals that the accountability bottleneck is federal evidence-withholding, not immunity doctrine. Expect Morgan's defense to invoke Supremacy Clause immunity and attempt removal to federal court.
