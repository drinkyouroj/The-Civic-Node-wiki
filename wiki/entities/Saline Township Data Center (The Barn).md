## Overview
A ~$16 billion AI data center campus under construction in Saline Township (Washtenaw County, outside Ann Arbor), built by Related Digital and Blackstone for Oracle as part of the OpenAI/Oracle [[Stargate]] program, nicknamed "The Barn." It draws more than 1 GW — about 25% of [[DTE Energy]]'s current peak — making it the single load reshaping DTE's Michigan rate base. It is the concrete data center behind the flagship's corrected claim that the cost of the AI boom reaches a billing address rather than a location.

## Key Facts
- **$16B** investment; **>1 GW** draw (~**25%** of DTE's current peak; ~1,383 MW at full buildout per related coverage vs. DTE's ~10.7 GW projected 2026 peak). [[Saline Township Data Center Construction Underway — Michigan Public - 2026-04-25]]
- Developers: **Related Digital + Blackstone**; tenant: **Oracle**; power: **DTE Energy**.
- Location: Saline Township, **Washtenaw County** — ~3 counties and 90+ miles from St. Clair County, on the opposite (SW) end of DTE's territory.
- Construction underway (began Q1 2026; targeting completion Q4 2027). Stated benefits: 2,500 union construction jobs, 450 on-site jobs, 1,500 county-wide, $14M community benefits, 750 acres preserved.
- AG [[Dana Nessel]] has appealed the [[Michigan Public Service Commission]]'s **conditional approval of DTE's power-supply contracts** for the campus, citing redactions and insufficient transparency.

## Newsletter Relevance
The counter-case to the wiki's [[Stargate]] "announced ≠ built" thesis: Saline is financed and under construction. But the fight simply moved downstream — from "will it get built" to "who pays for the grid it needs," now litigated through the [[Dana Nessel]] appeal and DTE's rate case. It is the proof object for [[AI Cost Incidence]] localized to Michigan, and the real-world referent that made the flagship's "two counties over" line false (it's three counties and on the far side of the state).
