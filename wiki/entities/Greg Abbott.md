## Overview
Republican Governor of Texas. Signed the redrawn pro-Republican U.S. House map in August 2025 — passed by the GOP-led state legislature after [[Donald Trump]] prodded Texas Republicans to redistrict mid-decade for partisan advantage. The U.S. Supreme Court formally reinstated the map on April 27, 2026 (6-3, formalizing a December 2025 interim ruling), reversing a lower court's finding that it was likely racially discriminatory.

## Key Facts
- Signed Texas U.S. House map August 2025; legislature is Republican-led
- The map could flip up to 5 currently Democratic-held U.S. House seats to Republicans in the November 2026 midterms — per [[SCOTUS Reinstates Texas Voting Map — Reuters]]
- The Texas redraw triggered a symmetric California Democratic redraw (5 more D seats, allowed by SCOTUS in February 2026)
