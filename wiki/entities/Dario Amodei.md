## Overview

CEO and co-founder of Anthropic. Former VP of Research at OpenAI. In 2026, the central figure in the conflict between Anthropic and the US Defense Department over military AI use. Scheduled to visit the UK in late May 2026, where the British government plans to pitch him on expanding Anthropic's UK presence.

## Key Facts

- CEO of [[Anthropic]]
- UK government plans to present expansion proposals to him during a late May 2026 visit [[Britain woos Anthropic expansion after US defence clash]]
- Presiding over Anthropic's legal fight against DoD blacklisting
- Authored Anthropic's official statement on the DoD conflict, articulating two red lines: mass domestic surveillance and fully autonomous weapons [[Statement from Dario Amodei on our discussions with the Department of War]]
