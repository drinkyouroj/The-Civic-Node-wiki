## Overview
Attorney General of Florida. In April 2026 opened the first U.S. state-level criminal investigation into a frontier AI lab, targeting OpenAI over ChatGPT's alleged role in the April 2025 Florida State University mass shooting. Previously launched a civil investigation on April 9, 2026; escalated to criminal on April 21, 2026.

## Key Facts
- Announced criminal investigation of OpenAI April 21, 2026, with subpoenas for internal training materials, user-threat-handling policies, crime-reporting policies, organizational chart, and ChatGPT employee list (covering March 2024 – April 2026). [[Florida AG Criminal Investigation into OpenAI — NBC News]]
- Frames ChatGPT's output to the FSU shooter as potentially criminal: "If this were a person on the other side of the screen, we would be charging them with murder."
- Self-positions as "limited government" proponent with narrow interference threshold ("significant harm to our people") — the framing leaves space for AI companies to invoke limited-government principles in defense.
- Works in parallel with Governor [[Ron DeSantis]]'s AI Bill of Rights proposal (December 2025).
