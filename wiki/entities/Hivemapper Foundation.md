## Overview
The Hivemapper Foundation is the governance and incentive-administration arm of the [[Hivemapper]] decentralized mapping network. The Foundation directs the network's [[HONEY]] token rewards and sets coverage priorities through governance directives. The for-profit operating company is **Bee Maps**, which raised $32M in 2026 to scale enterprise mapping products built atop the network. The Foundation–Bee Maps split mirrors the [[Helium Foundation]] / [[Nova Labs]] structure in the broader DePIN sector: a nominally decentralized governance entity sets reward direction; an operating company captures upside on the data being produced.

## Key Facts

### May 2, 2026 — 10M HONEY coverage-map directive
- **Announcement (May 2, 2026)**: Foundation committed **"at least 10 million HONEY"** in extra contributor incentives, directed at "green" and "orange" roads, through **June 30, 2026**. Announcement made via Hivemapper's official X account (post ID 2050688549612384573).
- **In-app coverage map**: Foundation launched a live coverage map that classifies every road into three priority buckets:
  - **green** = cover ASAP
  - **orange** = needs coverage
  - **teal** = good for now
- **Stated mechanism**: contributors driving green and orange roads earn the additional HONEY rewards through June 30. The Foundation alone selects which roads belong to which bucket; operators decide where they drive.
- **Buy-pressure framing — fact-check note**: third-party aggregator coverage (CoinMarketCap CMC AI's "Latest Updates" page) characterizes the program as "designed to increase coverage and create buy pressure on HONEY." Important distinction: **this framing is the aggregator's analytical paraphrase, NOT a direct Foundation quote** as of the verified-sources pass on 2026-05-21. The Foundation's own May 2 announcement language available in aggregator paraphrase frames the program as a coverage-acceleration tool; the explicit "buy pressure" attribution is editorial.
- **Approximate USD value at announcement**: 10M HONEY ≈ $23,700 at May 2 prices per CoinMarketCap. (Modest dollar value; the structural significance is the operator-direction precedent, not the absolute reward size.)

Two analytical lines this entity supports:

1. **The "Foundation directs, operators decide" pattern** — Foundations in DePIN sit at the reward-direction layer with effectively unilateral authority. The institutional infrastructure available to contributors to push back ranges from weak (Helium's veHNT-with-proxy-concentration) to non-existent (Hivemapper's directive-only structure). This is the editorial spine of the "[[Franchise vs. Business]]" concept argued in the Helium flagship.
2. **The buildout-era reward-control question** — During an AI buildout that creates pricing power throughout the supply chain, who captures the windfall depends on what institutional infrastructure exists at the labor / contributor layer. The [[Samsung]] union extracted 12% of operating profit via organized labor leverage. Hivemapper contributors got a directive about which roads to drive. Same buildout-era cause, asymmetric institutional response.
