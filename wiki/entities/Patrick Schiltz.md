## Overview
Patrick J. Schiltz is the **Chief U.S. District Judge for the District of Minnesota**. During [[Operation Metro Surge]] he produced the documentary record of ICE's defiance of the federal courts — first finding that ICE had violated **96 court orders in 74 cases**, then, after the U.S. Attorney accused the court of inaccuracy, ordering a re-verification that **confirmed the count and added 113 more violations**, bringing the documented total to roughly **210**. His orders contain the sharpest judicial language on record about the operation.

## Key Facts
- The findings came in an individual habeas case, ***Juan T.R. v. Noem***, **No. 0:26-cv-00107 (PJS/DLM)** — **not** the high-profile state suit to halt the operation (that was *State of Minnesota v. Noem*, before Judge [[Katherine Menendez]]). Prior wiki notes conflated the two; this is the correction.
- **Jan. 28, 2026 (ECF No. 10)**: canceled a contempt show-cause hearing for Acting ICE Director [[Todd Lyons]] after ICE released the detainee, but attached an appendix documenting "96 court orders that ICE has violated in 74 cases" since Jan. 1, 2026 — and called the count "almost certainly substantially understated."
- **Feb. 26, 2026 (ECF No. 12)**: after U.S. Attorney **Daniel Rosen** (Feb. 9 email) called the finding "far beyond the pale of accuracy," Schiltz had other judges and his clerks re-verify: **97 violations in 66 cases** confirmed (Appendix A), plus **113 additional violations in 77 additional cases** (Appendix B), most occurring *after* the Jan. 28 order. Threatened escalation to **criminal contempt**.
- Praised the DOJ civil-division attorneys who resigned (including "unfortunately, Ana Voss") and blamed DOJ leadership and "the Administration sending 3000 ICE agents to Minnesota … without making any provision for handling the hundreds of lawsuits."

## Newsletter Relevance
Schiltz is the human-scale figure for the **rule-of-law** thread under the institutional-breakdown story: a Republican-appointed chief judge (appointed by George W. Bush) driven to threaten criminal contempt against the executive branch — repeatedly — to force compliance. His "ICE is not a law unto itself" framing is the cleanest available articulation of the stakes, and his orders are the primary-source counterweight to the [[Enforcement Theater]] narrative.

## Quotes
