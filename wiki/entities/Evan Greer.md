## Overview
Evan Greer is the director (formerly deputy director) of [[Fight for the Future]], a queer- and trans-led US digital rights organization. Public face of the most consistent organizational opposition to [[KOSA]], [[Section 230]] sunset proposals, [[Age Verification]] laws, and the broader [[Bad Internet Bills Campaign]] cluster. Authored or co-authored several of the canonical opposition op-eds across Vice, Teen Vogue, and other publications. Trans/queer; identifies as parent of a tween (per Vice 2022 op-ed).

## Key Facts
- Authored the canonical KOSA-opposition op-ed: [[LGBTQ Youth Are Under Attack — Why Are Democrats Pushing a Bill That Hurts Them More]] (Vice, December 2022) — the original formal articulation of the duty-of-care critique
- Co-authored the most current consolidated argument: [[Why Are Some Democrats Backing MAGA's Anti-LGBTQ+ Censorship?]] (Teen Vogue, October 2025) — the three-vector framing (Section 230, age verification, KOSA)
- Frequently quoted across The Verge, Vox, WaPo, Tech Policy Press as digital rights expert
- Has been **publicly smeared by Sen. [[Richard Blumenthal]]** as part of "a pawn of Big Tech" — Blumenthal's response to FtF's KOSA opposition; widely seen as a particularly cynical attack on a queer-/trans-led organization
- Argues for affirmative alternatives: federal data privacy law (ADPPA), antitrust (AICOA, Open App Markets Act), algorithmic justice — "rather than throwing trans kids and human rights under the bus"
