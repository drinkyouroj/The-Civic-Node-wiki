## Overview

Anonymous UK-based actor (claims Oxford, England, claims name "Ben Conway" — real identity unverified) who ran a multi-year catfishing operation against [[Steven K. Bonnell II]] using the stolen identity and photos of minor [[Rose (AH)]]. Solo is the proximate cause of the harm at the center of [[Doe v. Bonnell (1-25-cv-20757)]] but is not a party to the suit. He is jurisdictionally unreachable by US civil process and operates under multiple pseudonyms.

## Key Facts

- **Known aliases:** Solo, Solo Leveling, SoloTinyLeaks (KiwiFarms), RoseOfTheGarden (Discord), RoseIsCumming (Twitter, Cash App), Ben Conway (Snapchat claim), ELOhero5 (old Snapchat), "Lauren" (per Destiny's document production SB 001910 references a relationship with a woman named Lauren — unclear if connected).
- **Claimed location:** Oxford, England. Evidence supporting this: British accent throughout relationship with Rose, European date format visible in screenshots, bio listing tip amounts in British pounds (on the @RoseIsCumming Twitter).
- **Claimed age:** 21 when he met Rose in November 2021; likely 21-22 during the active Destiny operation in 2022.
- **Met Rose:** November 2021, on Yubo (an app marketed to under-18s). Rose was 17.
- **Method:** Created a Discord account with Rose's name and photos (without her credentials or consent), created a Twitter account "RoseIsCumming" impersonating her (bio listed age 18), initiated contact with Destiny in April 2022 posing as an 18-year-old fan.
- **Operation scale:** Over roughly 7 months (April-November 2022), exchanged ~300 pages of Discord messages plus videos with Destiny. Rose had no access to the account; Solo occasionally screenshotted messages to her "to brag."
- **Threats against Rose:** When Rose tried to end the relationship in August 2022, Solo threatened to kill her family and to "kidnap her and use her as a sex slave." He continued contact attempts through new accounts for years.
- **The leak:** November 29, 2024 — posted 26 videos plus ~300 pages of Discord logs to KiwiFarms under the handle SoloTinyLeaks.
- **Manufactured consent:** In December 2024 KiwiFarms posts, claimed Rose had "given me the account a while ago" and that she "doesn't really care about stuff being leaked." Under sworn deposition, Rose contradicted both claims.
- **Motivation:** Rose's deposition speculation (cross-examination by defense counsel): Ben "liked his content, like his streams" and as a bisexual man "may have derived sexual pleasure from it."
- **Post-leak contact:** February 2025 — reached Rose again via a Snapchat account labeled "Ben Conway." She blocked him.
- **Possible connection to "Callum":** Rose speculated Callum (who informed her of the leak via email starting August 26, 2025) may have been connected to Pxie's circle. This remains unconfirmed.

## Newsletter Relevance

Solo is the structural center of [[You Can't Sue the Catfish]] and of the [[Reachability Routing]] concept. He caused every identifiable harm in the chain — the identity theft of Rose, the years-long coercion of a minor, the deception of Destiny, the fabrication of consent, the leak itself — and none of it generates civil liability he can be made to pay. The legal system cannot reach him. So it reaches Destiny instead.
