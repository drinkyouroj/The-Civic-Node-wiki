## Overview
Corey Lewandowski is a senior adviser to DHS Secretary [[Kristi Noem]] in Trump's second administration. Former 2016 Trump campaign manager, longtime political operative. Lewandowski's role at DHS is documented in two distinct accountability threads: (1) [[Gregory Bovino]] reported directly to Noem AND to Lewandowski, an unusual political-operations reporting line for a Border Patrol commander; and (2) Lewandowski is among the four named senior DHS officials whose text-message preservation DHS has been unable to confirm in the [[American Oversight]] FOIA lawsuit over [[Operation Metro Surge]]-period communications.

## Key Facts
- Senior adviser to DHS Secretary Kristi Noem
- Former Trump 2016 campaign manager; political operative across multiple Republican campaigns
- **Bovino reporting line**: Per NBC News, [[Gregory Bovino]] "reported directly to Noem and her senior adviser Corey Lewandowski" — the political-operations chain of command for a Border Patrol commander overseeing federal enforcement operations [[Border Patrol's Gregory Bovino to retire, sources say]]
- **Records preservation question**: Named in DHS court filings as one of the senior officials (with Noem, Deputy Sec [[Troy Edgar]], senior adviser Rob Law) whose text-message preservation under the Federal Records Act has not been confirmed [[DHS Admits It Provided Erroneous Information on Texts of Noem and DHS Brass]]
- DHS disabled its automatic text-archiving system in April 2025; manual preservation is now the protocol; DHS has not confirmed whether Lewandowski has followed it

## Newsletter Relevance
Lewandowski is the structural connection between two accountability threads that look separate but are the same: the operational decision chain (Bovino → Lewandowski/Noem) and the records concealment chain (Lewandowski/Noem → disabled archive system → "erroneous" claim about preservation).
