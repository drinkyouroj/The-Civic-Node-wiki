## Overview
31-year-old Torrance, California man charged in the [[White House Correspondents' Dinner Shooting 2026]] (April 25, 2026). Caltech-educated mechanical engineer and computer scientist, part-time tutor at [[C2 Education]], self-employed game developer. Allegedly charged through a Secret Service checkpoint at the [[Washington Hilton]] armed with a shotgun, handgun, and multiple knives; was tackled (not shot) by Secret Service. A manifesto sent to family members before the attack styled him the "Friendly Federal Assassin."

- Worked part-time for [[C2 Education]] in Torrance; named "Teacher of the Month" December 2024
- Previously a mechanical engineer at IJK Controls (South Pasadena) and a Caltech TA; won a 2016 Caltech robotics competition
