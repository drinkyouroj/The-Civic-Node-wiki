## Overview

Google's AI research division (formed by the 2023 merger of Google Brain and DeepMind). One of the three dominant US frontier-AI labs alongside [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]].

## Key Facts

- Has previously worked on table tennis robotics, per AP reporting on Sony's 2026 Ace announcement [[Sony Ace Table Tennis Robot Beats Human Pros — AP]]
