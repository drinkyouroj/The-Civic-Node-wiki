## Overview

"Rose" is the pseudonym for a young woman in Illinois whose identity was stolen by her then-boyfriend [[Solo]] (Ben Conway), a UK-based adult male. Solo created fake accounts impersonating Rose across Discord, Twitter, and Cash App, used them to catfish [[Destiny]] for years, and coerced Rose into creating explicit content under threats of death and kidnapping. Rose testified at a deposition on October 23, 2025 (Rockford, Illinois) in the case Jane Doe v. Steven K. Bonnell II. Her testimony was devastating for [[Pixie]]'s case — she confirmed she never communicated with Destiny, directly or indirectly.

## Key Facts

- Real identity: Under seal; known as "Rose" in proceedings
- Location: Illinois
- Age: Was a minor (under 18) during part of 2022 based on birth certificate produced (DOB Oct 30, 2004), though the exact timeline of when Solo used her identity vs. her age is disputed in filings
- Met Solo: November 2021, on an app designed for underage children
- Deposition date: October 23, 2025, Rockford, Illinois
- Deposition context: Destiny's side subpoenaed her after Kiwi Farms doxed her identity; members of "Justical's Discord server" harassed her and her family via multiple email accounts until she agreed to cooperate with Pixie's attorney (Joan Peters)

## Deposition Key Testimony

- Never communicated with Destiny electronically or by phone
- Did not control the Discord, Twitter, or Cash App accounts Solo created in her name
- Did not know who Pixie was and had never seen Pixie's videos
- Solo threatened to kill himself, kill her family, and kidnap her as a sex slave to coerce her into creating explicit content
- Solo told her he was 21-22 and was a grown adult at the time
- Solo's UK location explains the European date format timestamps on all leaked material
- Rose broke away from Solo approximately August 2023 — explaining why her "account" stopped communicating with Destiny around that time
- Rose had seen some of the Discord messages but only after Solo shared them with her; she was "in the loop" to a limited extent but was not the person communicating

## Why She Matters

Rose is both a victim and the key witness that collapsed Pixie's central factual premise. Pixie's lawsuit rested on an alleged relationship between Destiny and Rose. The deposition revealed there was no such relationship — only a catfish operation by Solo. Rose's testimony also provided evidence of Solo's identity, UK location, and predatory behavior that could be relevant to any future criminal or civil action against Solo.

Destiny's attorney's words to her post-deposition: "I'm sorry you had to go through that... No child should have to go through that."
