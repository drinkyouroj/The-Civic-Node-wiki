## Overview
Adaspace Technology Co., Ltd. — a Chengdu-based commercial aerospace enterprise leading the construction of the "star compute" satellite constellation, a 2,800-satellite [[Space-Based Computing]] network for AI inference and training. First batch launched May 2025; goal is 1,000-satellite scale by 2030, full 2,800 by 2035.

## Key Facts
- Headquarters: Chengdu, Sichuan Province, China
- Executive VP: Zhao Hongjie
- "Star compute" plan: 2,400 inference + 400 training computing satellites in dawn-dusk, sun-synchronous, and low-inclination orbits at 500–1,000 km
- Compute targets: hundred-thousand-petaflop inference; million-petaflop training
- May 14, 2025: first satellite batch launched from [[Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center]]
- November 2025: deployed [[Alibaba]]'s Qwen3 LLM in orbit — claimed first ever in-orbit deployment of a general-purpose LLM (in-orbit inference < 2 minutes)
- Collaboration with [[Shanghai Jiao Tong University]]: world's first space-based-compute-controlled ground robot demonstration
- March 26, 2026: launched **Prometheus** — commercial space-computing cloud platform, with MIIT's small/medium-enterprise development promotion center
- Per CAICT: Chinese space-compute industry projected at ~¥250B (~$36.6B) by 2030

## Newsletter Relevance
The architectural workaround to the [[AI DRAM Crisis|terrestrial-compute chokepoints]] the wiki has documented. While [[OpenAI]] locks up [[Samsung]] / [[SK Hynix]] DRAM and Qatar's [[Helium]] production goes offline, ADAspace is building compute infrastructure that sits *outside* the terrestrial supply chain entirely. This is structural, not rhetorical, response to U.S. export controls and chokepoint pressure. Whether the timelines hold is the open question; the milestones already achieved (Qwen3 in orbit, Prometheus launch, robot control) are technically significant.
