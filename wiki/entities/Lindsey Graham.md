## Overview
Lindsey Graham (R-SC) is a U.S. Senator and one of the chamber's most hawkish voices on Iran. In the wiki he is the third member of the Iran Senate-hawk bloc (with [[Roger Wicker]] and [[Ted Cruz]]) pressing the Trump administration for renewed strikes, and the longtime lead sponsor of the [[EARN IT Act]] in the internet-bills cluster.

## Key Facts
- **Iran maximalism (2026):** Graham has called for fresh US strikes on Iran's energy infrastructure; asked whether operations should be "bigger or smaller," he said "I'm in the camp of bigger," and urged "unleashing holy hell" via a "massive wave of military, cyber and psychological attacks" on the regime. ([C-SPAN](https://www.c-span.org/event/news-conference/sen-lindsey-graham-speaks-to-press-on-iran-strikes/440876), [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/graham-suggests-trump-help-iran-protesters-military-cyber-psychological-attacks-against-regime))
- **"Poison pill":** critics argue Graham is pushing Trump toward conditions that would sink the draft Iran deal — triggering a MAGA-base revolt over renewed war. ([Raw Story](https://www.rawstory.com/lindsey-graham-2676978035/), [Salon](https://www.salon.com/2026/03/14/lindsey-graham-just-triggered-a-maga-revolt-over-iran/))
- **Senate-hawk-bloc dissent (May 22, 2026):** named with [[Ted Cruz]] as a parallel skeptic to [[Roger Wicker]]'s public rejection of Trump's draft deal — the first publicly-staged intra-GOP Senate-hawk dissent since the April ceasefire. [[Wicker Warns Trump Against Ill Advised Iran Deal — The Hill - 2026-05-22]]
- **Internet bills:** longtime lead sponsor of the [[EARN IT Act]], the [[Section 230]] carve-out the wiki's [[Bad Internet Bills Campaign]] cluster treats as a censorship vector adjacent to [[KOSA]] and [[SESTA-FOSTA|SESTA/FOSTA]].

## Newsletter Relevance
Graham is a two-cluster figure: the **Iran [[Coalition Fracture]]** (the GOP Senate hawks publicly breaking with Trump's diplomatic track — a rare staged intra-party dissent the wiki tracks as a power story) and the **internet-bills / speech** cluster (EARN IT as a [[Duty of Care (Internet Bills)|duty-of-care]]-style pressure on platforms). His "bigger" Iran posture is the maximalist pole against which Trump's dual-track messaging is measured — and a reminder that the same senators pushing speech-protection bills also push the most aggressive war posture.
