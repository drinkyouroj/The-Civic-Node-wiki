## Summary
This is the official [[Department of Homeland Security|DHS]] press release establishing the "worst of the worst" framing for [[Operation Metro Surge]]. Secretary [[Kristi Noem]] claimed ICE had arrested 3,000 "criminal illegal aliens including vicious murderers, rapists, child pedophiles" in Minneapolis over roughly six weeks. The release offered no statistical breakdown of arrestees' records — only a handful of named worst-case profiles — making it the rhetorical claim that the later FOIA data (see Pages 1 and 2) directly contradicts.

## Key Points
- **PRIMARY government source.** DHS / Secretary [[Kristi Noem]] claimed that in ~6 weeks ICE "arrested 3,000 criminal illegal aliens including vicious murderers, rapists, child pedophiles" on Minneapolis streets.
- DHS also claimed **more than 10,000 criminal illegal aliens arrested overall** (a broader national claim beyond Minneapolis).
- **NO statistical record breakdown was provided** — the release featured only **roughly 13 named "worst-case" individual profiles** chosen to illustrate the framing.
- Companion DHS release (**2026-02-03**): "DHS Arrests More Criminal Illegal Aliens in Minnesota, Including Terrorist, Child [Predator]" — https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/02/03/dhs-arrests-more-criminal-illegal-aliens-minnesota-including-terrorist-child
- The "worst of the worst" / "murderers, rapists, child pedophiles" language is the through-line of DHS messaging for the operation.

## Newsletter Angles
- **This is the claim, not the evidence.** DHS asserted "3,000 criminal illegal aliens" and named ~13 people; it never released the aggregate record data. When the [[Deportation Data Project]] forced that data out via FOIA, fewer than one-quarter had any conviction. The juxtaposition — official adjectives vs. the dataset — is the spine of the arrest-demographics section.
- **Anecdote-as-statistic.** Thirteen profiles standing in for 3,000 arrests is the rhetorical move worth naming: the worst case is presented as representative.
- **Government as its own least reliable narrator.** The same agency that called arrestees "the worst of the worst" produced (under legal compulsion) the data that refutes the label.
