## Summary

Long Sprinklr marketing-blog post breaking down how 2025 social media algorithms work across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Reddit, and YouTube. Positions algorithms as "black boxes" of deep-learning models trained on massive engagement data, then offers tactical guidance for marketers. The piece is simultaneously the most detailed summary of the "2025 algorithm" marketing consensus and a sales pitch for Sprinklr Social.

## Key Points

- **Data scale**: cites 2ZB (2010) → 181ZB (2025) growth in data generated annually as the fuel for increasingly personalized ranking.
- **Instagram 2025**: intent modeling (Meta AI), content score weighting (saves > shares > comments > likes), format weight (Reels > Carousels > Stories > Images), Threads activity crossover influence, multimodal ranking (indexing video, text overlays, and spoken content).
- **Facebook 2025**: four-step pipeline (Inventory → Signals → Predictions → Score), "smart chrono mode" blending chronology with engagement probability, AI moderator assist boosting visibility of posts flagged as educational/health/public-interest.
- **TikTok 2025**: For You Feed based on content-similarity not social graph; first 3 seconds of watch time critical; rewards content quality over account size.
- **LinkedIn 2025**: "Creator Mode 2.0" with real-time topic insights; "conversation health score" penalizing low-effort comments.
- **Reddit 2025**: "Hot" shifting to personalized "Best" sort (started Feb 2025); engagement over freshness; algorithm tuned for dwell time, which the piece notes may promote "controversial or slightly older" content.
- **YouTube 2025**: cascading-filter model that tests new videos on small audiences before expansion; Shorts use a separate algorithm; "Collab Connect" tags favor creator-to-creator collaborations.
- Busts common myths: posting frequency ≠ reach; AI-only content underperforms; cross-platform copy-paste fails.

## Newsletter Angles

- **"Algorithmic Darwinism" framing** captured in the piece is the cleanest one-liner on the attention-economy logic: only content that is "relevant, timely, and personalized" survives. Direct hook for [[Attention Economy]] and [[Algorithmic Incentives]].
- **Reddit's "Best" sort pivot** is the most newsworthy specific claim in the piece — a structural shift from transparent community ranking to personalized ranking that increases dwell time while surfacing more controversial content. This is a concrete instance of [[Algorithmic Radicalization]] dynamics being baked into a platform that used to be the exception.
- **The marketing advice to "create emotionally engaging or sticky conversations... even if slightly provocative or counter-narrative"** is the attention economy's incentive structure in plain English. Worth quoting.
- **181ZB statistic** is a useful scale number for any piece on surveillance capitalism or data infrastructure.
