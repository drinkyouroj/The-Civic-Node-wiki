## Summary
On the morning of April 23, 2026 — one day after Iran fired on three ships and seized two in the Strait of Hormuz — President Trump issued a Truth Social post ordering the U.S. Navy to "shoot and kill any boat, small boats though they may be… that is putting mines" in the strait, and announced intensified mine-clearing operations. The order is the sharpest public escalation of rules of engagement since the Feb. 28 opening attacks.
