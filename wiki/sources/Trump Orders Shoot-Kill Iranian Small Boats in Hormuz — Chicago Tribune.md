## Summary
On the morning of April 23, 2026 — one day after IRGC forces fired on three ships and seized two in the Strait of Hormuz — President Trump issued a Truth Social post ordering the U.S. Navy to "shoot and kill any boat, small boats though they may be… that is putting mines" in the strait, and announced intensified mine-clearing operations. The order is the sharpest public escalation of rules of engagement since the Feb. 28 opening attacks.

## Key Points
- "Shoot and kill" as a presidential rules-of-engagement order delivered via social media is a documentary first. Whether the Navy issues a matching fragmentary order or treats the statement as political signal rather than operational directive is the open question.
- The post frames targets as mine-layers specifically, implying intelligence-driven targeting — but "small boats though they may be" suggests a looser threshold. The gap between the two framings is the ROE question.
- Mine-clearing intensification is the practical response to the previous day's three-ship attack ([[Iran Fires on 3 Ships in Strait of Hormuz — AP]]); the political escalation is the rhetorical layer on top.
