## Summary

PBS NewsHour / Associated Press report, published January 8, 2026, documenting Minnesota Public Safety Commissioner Bob Jacobson's on-the-record acknowledgment that prosecuting the ICE agent who killed Renée Good would be "extremely difficult, if not impossible" without federal cooperation — cooperation that the FBI has withdrawn. Primary source for the Jacobson quote.

## Key Points

- **Jacobson verbatim**: "For us to be able to do that, it would be extremely difficult, if not impossible, without cooperation from the federal government."
- **Jacobson's role**: Minnesota Public Safety Commissioner.
- **Standard practice**: Minnesota state agents "all the time" work with the FBI on joint investigations; the FBI typically holds the original investigative notes, reports, and forensic evidence.
- **Current state**: "We have none of that." State investigators have been cut off from the case file.
- **Reversal**: The FBI initially agreed to a joint framework with the Minnesota BCA, the U.S. Attorney's office in Minneapolis, and the Hennepin County Attorney's office, then reversed course.
