## Summary
Teen Vogue op-ed (October 2025) — the most recent and most consolidated KOSA / Section 230 / age-verification opposition argument in this cluster, written under the second Trump administration after the right's "obsessive rush to blame trans people for Charlie Kirk's death." Greer and Rose name **three Democratic-enabled vectors of anti-LGBTQ+ censorship**: (1) Section 230 sunset proposals (Durbin co-sponsoring, Klobuchar calling it "a problem for our democracy" the day before ABC suspended Jimmy Kimmel); (2) age verification laws (Kansas's broad "harmful to minors" definition includes nonsexual "acts of homosexuality"); (3) reintroduced KOSA (Schumer co-sponsoring; FTC under Trump now hosting anti-trans "workshops"). Frames Sen. Blumenthal's smear of FtF as "a pawn of Big Tech" and contrasts with GLAAD's renewed concerns about FTC weaponization.

## Key Points
- **Three vectors** of Democratic-enabled censorship:
  1. **Section 230 sunset:** Durbin co-sponsored a previous version; Klobuchar in Sep 2025 Politico interview called Section 230 "a problem for our democracy" — the day before ABC suspended Jimmy Kimmel.
  2. **Age verification laws:** Kansas's law defines "harmful to minors" to include nonsexual "acts of homosexuality." Online ID checks expand surveillance and enable government targeting.
  3. **KOSA reintroduction:** Most recent version co-sponsored by Schumer; Blumenthal continues to push.
- **Trump FTC weaponization:** July 2025 FTC anti-trans "workshop" exploring how to use agency authority against gender-affirming-care providers; Request for Public Comment claiming consumers "may have been exposed to false or unsupported claims" about gender-affirming care.
- **17 state AGs** opposed FTC's unsupported claims (Assigned Media, 2025).
- **Tesla Takedown organizers** sent letter to Durbin (April 2025) explaining how Section 230 sunset would kneecap their organizing.
- **CBS settled $16M** with Trump admin rather than fight a winnable defamation case — example of corporate capitulation that Section 230 sunset would amplify.
- **Project 2025** describes all queer/trans content as "pornography"; proposes criminalizing distribution.
- **Michigan bill (Sep 2025)** would ban any "depiction, description or simulation" of trans people as inherently "pornographic."
- **GLAAD shifted position:** Told WaPo (June 2025) that they have "renewed concerns" about KOSA given Trump-FTC weaponization.
- **Stonewall monument** had "T" removed from "LGBT" by Trump admin.
- **Blumenthal** smeared FtF as "a pawn of Big Tech" — notable rhetorical attack on a queer-/trans-led group.
