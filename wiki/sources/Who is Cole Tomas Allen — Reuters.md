## Summary
Reuters profile of [[Cole Tomas Allen]], the 31-year-old Torrance, California man identified as the suspect in the April 25, 2026 shooting at the [[White House Correspondents' Dinner Shooting 2026|White House Correspondents' Dinner]] at the Washington Hilton. Allen graduated from [[Caltech]] in 2017 (mechanical engineering) and earned a master's in computer science from CSU Dominguez Hills in 2025. He worked part-time as a tutor for [[C2 Education]] (named "Teacher of the Month" December 2024 at the Torrance office) and as a self-employed game developer. LinkedIn self-description: "mechanical engineer and computer scientist by degree, independent game developer by experience, teacher by birth."

## Key Points
- Suspect identified as Cole Tomas Allen, ~31, of Torrance, CA — a coastal South Bay community adjacent to LA
- BS Mechanical Engineering, Caltech 2017 (school confirmed a person of that name graduated that year); MS Computer Science, CSU Dominguez Hills 2025
- Worked for [[C2 Education]] (a national private tutoring service); previously a mechanical engineer at IJK Controls in South Pasadena and a Caltech TA
- "Causes" listed only as "Science and Technology"
- Armed with a shotgun when he opened fire at a Secret Service agent in the Hilton — outside the ballroom where [[Donald Trump]], [[Melania Trump]], [[JD Vance]], and several cabinet secretaries were attending
- Police chief said suspect was a guest at the Hilton; no motive yet determined as of this filing
