## Summary

On Easter Sunday, April 5, Trump posted a profanity-laden ultimatum to Iran on Truth Social threatening to destroy power plants and bridges on Tuesday if the Strait of Hormuz remained closed. He set an 8:00 PM Eastern deadline. The threats followed a 48-hour rescue operation for two downed U.S. pilots inside Iran. Trump simultaneously signaled a deal was possible "by Monday" while threatening to "take" Iran's oil if talks failed.

## Key Points

- Truth Social post: "Tuesday will be Power Plant Day, and Bridge Day, all wrapped up in one, in Iran." Ended with "Praise be to Allah."
- Separate post set precise deadline: "Tuesday, 8:00 P.M. Eastern Time!"
- Fox News: "If they don't make a deal, I'm considering blowing everything up and taking over the oil."
- ABC News: "We're blowing up the whole country" and "very little" off the table.
- Two U.S. pilots rescued from Iranian territory; second "seriously wounded" — Trump called it "an Easter Miracle."
- U.S. gasoline hit $4.11/gallon (from below $3 when strikes on Iran began Feb. 28).
- Iran's Revolutionary Guards warned that attacks on U.S. economic interests would intensify if civilian targets hit again.
- 13 U.S. service members killed, hundreds wounded since strikes began.
- Iran had blocked the Strait after U.S.-Israeli attacks on Iran; Trump's stated strategic objectives "near completion."
