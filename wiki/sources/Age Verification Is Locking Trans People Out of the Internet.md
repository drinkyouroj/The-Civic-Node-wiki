## Summary
Tech Policy Press analysis argues that online [[age verification]] regimes sweeping the globe are structurally biased against trans people. Biometric and document-based systems built on legacy gender-coded infrastructure frequently misgender trans users, while hormone therapy alters facial soft tissue in ways age-estimation AI cannot handle. The piece frames age verification as a surveillance and censorship architecture that hits [[LGBTQ+ youth]] hardest in the same jurisdictions stripping trans rights more broadly.

## Key Points
- Current facial-recognition and biometric age-estimation tools show measurable, documented bias against trans people — they tend to misgender and misclassify age for transfeminine and transmasculine users on hormone therapy.
- Same US states passing [[age verification]] laws are also banning gender-affirming care, expelling trans students from bathrooms, and pursuing conversion therapy — the policy bundle is not coincidental.
- UK [[Online Safety Act]] rollout (July 2025) restricted Reddit health/advice forums, X and Reddit posts about Gaza and Ukraine, per BBC reporting. Ofcom discouraged some Reddit restrictions.
- Trans users face structural hurdles: out-of-date government IDs, inconsistent banking/credit access, biometric bias — meaning even trans adults over 18 get locked out.
- Data-breach risk is existential for trans users: [[Tea app breach]], [[Discord verification hack]], Tea driver's-license leak mapped to home addresses via 4chan.
- [[Fight for the Future]] and [[EFF]] frame this as lawmakers using "protecting kids" as cover to refuse accountability for Big Tech data practices.
- No evidence age verification has meaningfully reduced minors viewing porn; non-compliant sites grow traffic, [[VPN]] usage surges.

## Newsletter Angles
- The policy-technology convergence: the same administration that revoked trans passports is building black-box algorithmic identity verification with no appeal mechanism. This is Politics + Power + Technology & State all at once.
- [[Surveillance infrastructure]] framing: age verification isn't a feature, it's a new identity layer being bolted onto the internet — and it's being built by private vendors with no regulator oversight.
- The [[data broker]] pipeline to ICE: age verification data is only as safe as the weakest vendor. Tea breach shows what happens.
