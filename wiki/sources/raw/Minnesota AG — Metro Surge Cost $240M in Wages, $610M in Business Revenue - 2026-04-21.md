Attorney General Keith Ellison, along with Minneapolis and Saint Paul officials, filed an amended federal lawsuit against the Department of Homeland Security documenting severe economic harm from Operation Metro Surge.

## Wage Losses
- Minneapolis residents: $189.2 million in lost wages
- Saint Paul residents: $54.6 million in lost wages
- Total: over $240 million in lost wages

## Business Revenue Losses
- Minneapolis businesses: $444.8 million
- Saint Paul businesses: $165.4 million
- Total: approximately $610 million in lost revenue

## Survey Methodology
The U.S. Immigration Policy Center (USIPC) at University of California San Diego conducted surveys between February 17 and March 6, 2026:
- Individual respondents: 1,390 total (728 Minneapolis; 662 Saint Paul)
- Business respondents: approximately 900 (489 Minneapolis; 405 Saint Paul)
- Approximately 25% of individual respondents reported DHS interactions

The filing describes the USIPC work as "the most comprehensive economic assessment of the operation's impact to date."

## Key Quote
Ellison: "Operation Metro Surge caused severe harm to countless more residents of our state, to cities like Minneapolis and Saint Paul, and to the state of Minnesota itself."

## Additional Findings
- 60% of Minneapolis and 61% of Saint Paul businesses reported negative operational impact
- 62% of Minneapolis survey respondents with medical appointments who encountered DHS agents missed care
- 73.1% of Minneapolis respondents reported DHS agents did not show warrants
- Significant racial disparities documented in enforcement actions
