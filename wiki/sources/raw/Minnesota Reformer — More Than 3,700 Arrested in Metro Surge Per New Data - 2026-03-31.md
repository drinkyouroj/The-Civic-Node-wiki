KEY FACTS:
- More than 3,700 people arrested during Operation Metro Surge.
- Data obtained via Freedom of Information Act request, released through the Deportation Data Project. The dataset contains no names, but includes age, nationality, date, and location of each arrest.
- Less than one-quarter of those arrested had been convicted of a crime.
- 13% had pending criminal charges.
- LOAD-BEARING DEFINITION: ICE's definition of a "crime" includes misdemeanors, such as unauthorized entry into the country or traffic violations. (So the "convicted of a crime" denominator is not a "serious crime" denominator.)
- The data does NOT include arrests of U.S. citizens. The article cites a 2021 GAO audit finding that ICE does not systematically track its arrests of U.S. citizens.
- Majority of arrestees came from Ecuador and Mexico.
- People from Somalia made up less than 3% of arrests.
- About 15% of the records were missing the arrest state/location.
