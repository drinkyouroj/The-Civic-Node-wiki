## Summary

Marketing-agency blog post surveying expected 2025 changes to Facebook, Instagram, X, and Pinterest algorithms, with tactical advice for brands. Argues algorithms will weight "meaningful interactions," short-form video (Reels), and interest-based personalization more heavily, while becoming (claimed) more transparent to users.

## Key Points

- Facebook: shift toward longer comment threads, private-group shares, and continued push on Reels to compete with TikTok.
- Instagram: Reels dominant; DMs and comments weighted over likes; hashtag clusters and AI-driven suggested posts expanding.
- X: real-time topics, interest-based personalization, engagement-weighted ranking (likes, retweets, replies).
- Pinterest: image quality and topic clusters for discovery.
- Tactical prescriptions: engage meaningfully, prioritize short-form video, use hashtags strategically, stay consistent, leverage AI analytics, post interactive/behind-the-scenes/UGC content.

## Newsletter Angles

- Useful only as a snapshot of how marketers describe algorithm shifts — not as evidence for technical claims. The implicit analytical hook: marketers' tactical playbook reveals what platforms are actually rewarding (engagement theater, short-form video, parasocial behind-the-scenes content) and therefore what kind of civic information gets crowded out.
- Connects to [[Attention Economy]] and [[Algorithmic Incentives]]: everything a brand is coached to do here is legible to the same ranking systems that shape political discourse.
