## Summary
On April 22, 2026, IRGC naval forces fired on three cargo ships in the Strait of Hormuz — the Greek-owned Epaminondas, the Panama-flagged MSC Francesca (owned by MSC, the world's largest shipping company), and the Euphoria — and seized at least the first two, towing them to the Iranian coast. The attacks came one day after Trump extended a two-week ceasefire with Iran and maintained the US blockade of Iranian ports. Brent crude crossed $100/barrel, a 35% increase from prewar levels; the EU energy commissioner estimated disruption costs Europe €500M/day. Iran's FM called it impossible to reopen Hormuz given "blatant violations of the ceasefire."

## Key Points
- MSC Francesca was part of a larger MSC convoy; four other MSC vessels crossed by turning off their AIS transponders. Signal-blackout transit is becoming the standard defense mechanism, which has second-order regulatory and insurance consequences the wiki should track.
- Epaminondas's master reported UKMTO/Vanguard clearance to transit before IRGC opened fire — either a communication-chain failure or a deliberate Iranian signal that no clearance is valid.
- The Euphoria's attacker is unidentified in IRGC statements — possibly a separate actor or an IRGC unit disowning one ship. Unclear attribution is itself a tactic.
- US Central Command claims 28 vessels turned around or returned to Iranian ports under the blockade by April 21.
- Vortexa (analytics firm) recorded 34 sanctioned/Iranian-linked tanker movements in the week after the April 13 blockade; 6 confirmed outbound loaded (10.7M barrels of Iranian crude). Iran is still moving oil despite the blockade — important counterevidence to Trump's "collapsing financially" claim.
- Iranian chief negotiator Ghalibaf: reopening Hormuz is "not possible" given the US blockade amounts to "taking the global economy hostage."
- Pezeshkian (Iranian President) flagged the blockade and "breach of commitments" as obstacles to "genuine negotiations."
- Iran has not confirmed attendance at a second Pakistan-mediated talks round.
- Casualty count since Feb. 28: 3,375 in Iran, 2,290 in Lebanon, 23 in Israel, ~12 in Gulf Arab states, 15 IDF in Lebanon, 13 US service members.
