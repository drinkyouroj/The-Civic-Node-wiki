## Summary
Sunday Guardian (Indian outlet) tracks a viral conspiracy thread that emerged after the [[White House Correspondents' Dinner Shooting 2026]]: a 2023 social-media post by an account named "Henry Martinez" that mentioned "Cole Allen" resurfaced once [[Cole Tomas Allen]] was named as the suspect. The "Henry Martinez" account had no other visible activity. The thread merged with a separate discovery — a 2014 NASA technical publication authored by someone named Henry Martinez — and the fact that Allen had reportedly participated in a NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory summer research program during his Caltech years. None of these overlaps have been verified as connected; the article exists primarily to document the conspiracy formation in real time.
