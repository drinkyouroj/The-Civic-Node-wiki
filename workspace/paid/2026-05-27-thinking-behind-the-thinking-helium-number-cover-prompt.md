## Editorial notes on the variable choices

- **Anchor number `50%`**: the piece explicitly tells the reader which number to remember — *"The published piece carries both numbers: the 90.53% pass rate and the 50% top-two concentration. Most readers will remember the 50%."* That instruction also tells the cover designer what to anchor on. The 50% is the editorial signature; the 90% is the foil.
- **Anchor gloss `the room the pass rate hides`**: parallels the piece's closing rhetorical move — *"The 90% is the floor. The 50% is the room."* — without repeating it verbatim. Carries the same architectural metaphor.
- **Underline on `NUMBER`**: the noun being analytically corrected. Visually crosses out the "wrong" number so the margin can supply the right one.
- **Margin note `the 50% is the room.`**: lifts directly from the piece's closing line. Pairs with the underlined "NUMBER" — reader sees wrong number, gets right number in the margin. Five words, under the cursive legibility ceiling.
