## Analytical shift to convey

Started reading the Stargate cancellation as Force 4 of the DRAM story (a confirmation). 
Microsoft inheriting the site broke that frame. A stranded asset sits. This one got handed off 
three times in ninety days, each handoff an upgrade. Once the grid-position read landed, the 
whole AI capex analysis reorganized: chip layer vs. grid layer, different calendars, 
different decongestion dates.

## Hook options (from content plan item #18)

**Hook A:** I wrote two pieces about DRAM concentration in April. They were correct on their
own terms. They were also looking at the second-most-binding constraint and treating it as the
first.

**Hook B:** The thing that reorganized the analysis: chip supply is concentrated and slow, and
grid capacity is more concentrated and slower. Once you nest the two, the entire AI capex story
changes. The DRAM story still matters. It is just not the top of the constraint stack.

**Hook C:** What changed: I stopped writing about "AI buildout" as if compute was the binding
input. The binding input is power. The chip layer can be unconcentrated by China-side capacity
in 2028. The grid layer cannot be unconcentrated by anything on a comparable timeline.
