# Cover Image Prompt: The Bill of Rights Ends at the Contractor's Door (v2 — Handoff)

## Prompt

A cinematic editorial photograph in 16:9 landscape orientation, shot at chest-height with a medium-tight focal length, depicting two hands meeting in the act of passing a plain manila folder across the threshold of an open institutional doorway. The composition centers on the folder mid-transfer, both hands gripping it, the folder hovering directly above the line where the doorframe meets the floor.

The hand on the camera-side belongs to a person standing in a warm institutional interior: a formal dark navy or charcoal suit sleeve, a crisp white dress-shirt cuff with a single brass cufflink visible, the hand mature and steady, fingers gripping the long edge of the folder, the arm extended forward through the open doorframe. The hand on the other side belongs to a person standing in a cold modern workspace: a plain white dress-shirt sleeve with the cuff rolled up once, no jewelry, no formal markings, the hand younger or less formal, fingers closing on the opposite long edge of the folder, accepting the transfer. The two hands are gripping the folder simultaneously — the exact instant of the handoff. No faces, no torsos beyond the forearm — both figures are cropped at the elbow or just above.

The institutional side (left of frame): warm amber light spilling onto polished cream marble flooring, mahogany doorframe with brushed-brass kickplate, cream-painted plaster wall with subtle moulding, the suggestion of formal interior space beyond the figure (out of focus). The vendor side (right of frame): cold overhead fluorescent illumination on industrial polished concrete floor, the soft suggestion of office furniture and modern technology in the deep background — a server-rack silhouette, a desk edge, a small pole-mounted security camera in sharp peripheral focus — all rendered with the bluish-white cast of clinical lighting.

Lighting: a single hard warm key light (~3000K) from the camera-side at a forty-five-degree angle illuminating the formal sleeve and the folder's near edge, casting long shadows toward the doorway. The vendor side is lit entirely by ambient cool fluorescent (~5500K) — no warm light crosses the threshold. The two light temperatures meet precisely at the line of the doorframe, with the folder bisected by the temperature change: the near edge warm-lit, the far edge cool-lit, the handoff happening across the visible thermal boundary. Shallow depth of field with the folder and both gripping hands in sharpest focus; the deep background on both sides softened. Subtle film grain, editorial-magazine cover quality, no HDR glow, no post-processed halos.

The compositional argument: government does not perform the function. Government passes the function — physically, hand to hand — to a contractor at the threshold. The folder mid-transfer IS the bypass. The warm/cold color break across the folder IS the constitutional limit.

Aspect ratio: 16:9 landscape, 1456 × 816 pixels. For Midjourney / Nano Banana Pro: append `--ar 16:9`. For Flux / Stable Diffusion: set width 1456, height 816 explicitly. For GPT Image 1.5 / Seedream 5.0: include "16:9 aspect ratio, 1456 × 816 px".

## Remix notes

- **v1 → v2 pivot rationale:** v1 tried the "seal stops at the door" compression. Failed at render because (a) federal seals don't read as "constitutional limit" to a general viewer (they read as "official building"), (b) renders placed the seal freestanding in the marble rather than terminating at the doorframe, (c) the activity past the door read as generic office work without specifically signaling vendor-doing-constitutionally-bypassed-function. v2 puts the mechanism on the surface: government passes the function to a contractor. No iconography to decode; the action carries the argument.
- **Library scaffold used:** YouMind #13292 (Editorial Photograph Composition Prompt for Nano Banana Pro) — same structural template as v1 because the editorial-photography composition pattern (controlled palette, single hard key light, asymmetric balance, deep background context) still fits. The element mapping is different: v1 mapped subject→seal-on-marble; v2 maps subject→two-hands-and-folder-at-threshold. The warm/cold color-temperature contrast as carrying device is preserved across both versions.
- **Core DNA preserved (5/5):** photographic-cinematic register (Principle 4); warm/cold color-temperature contrast at the threshold carries the argument (Principle 5); single unified composition at thumbnail size — the folder-with-hands is the focal point (Principle 2); manila folder is plain and explicitly negative-prompted against text, no seal, no logos (Principle 3); the handoff IS the mechanism the piece names (Principle 1).
- **Why this compression is more legible than v1:**
  - The "handoff" is a universally readable action. The viewer does not need to know what a federal seal means; they recognize a hand passing something to another hand.
  - The mechanism the piece argues — government contracts a vendor to do what government couldn't do directly — is literally a handoff. The image is the mechanism.
  - At thumbnail size, two hands meeting over a folder reads instantly. Brass medallion on marble does not.
- **Render-time risks to watch for:**
  - **Hands rendering at different scales.** AI generators sometimes produce mismatched hand sizes. Both hands should be roughly the same size; the formal hand may be slightly larger if a depth cue is helpful, but a 2× size mismatch breaks the composition.
  - **Logos or text on the manila folder.** AI defaults toward putting text on documents and folders. Explicit negative-prompted but watch carefully. Acceptable to reroll if any text appears.
  - **Cufflink rendering as an insignia or seal.** A brass cufflink can pick up federal-agency styling if the model overreaches. Specify "plain brass cufflink, no engraving, no insignia."
  - **Skin-tone implications.** The prompt deliberately avoids implying ethnicity through the hand description. If renders consistently produce a same-race or politically-coded pairing, add explicit "neutral skin tones, racially ambiguous" or reroll until the pairing reads structural-not-demographic.
  - **Vendor-side background clutter.** The server-rack and camera in the deep background should be soft-focus suggestions, not detailed elements. If they compete with the handoff focal point, simplify to just an empty modern desk in the deep background.
  - **The folder reading as a briefcase or envelope.** Specify "plain manila document folder, unlabeled, US-letter sized" if the model substitutes a different document type. A briefcase weakens the argument because briefcases imply formal exchange; a manila folder implies routine paperwork — which is the editorial point (this handoff is routine, not ceremonial).
- **Deviation from $71B Bluff exemplar:** no face, no torso — just hands and a folder at a threshold. Closer in spirit to a hard-light commercial product shot than to a portrait.
- **Deviation from Atlanta exemplar:** no reflection device; warm/cold color-temperature contrast preserved but executed through an architectural temperature break rather than a reflective surface.
