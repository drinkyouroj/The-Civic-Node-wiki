# Cover Image Prompt: DHS Showed 9%. FOIA Showed Everyone.

## Prompt

A cinematic neo-noir editorial photograph, 16:9 landscape. At the front of an immense gathering of people, a small cluster of three or four anonymous figures in plain everyday clothing is carved out of the dark by a single hard overhead theatrical spotlight. They are the only thing fully revealed — sharp, crisp, almost clinically lit, their forms rendered in high-fidelity detail. Their faces are turned slightly away or left generic and unidentifiable; these are not specific people, just the few the light was pointed at.

Behind and around them stretches a vast crowd of ordinary people — laborers, a parent holding a small child, an elderly person, figures in workwear and everyday coats — extending back and dissolving into near-total blackness. Only a thin, cold blue rim of light grazes their shoulders and the edges of their faces, just enough that the eye reads them as an enormous human multitude rather than an empty void. The darkness swallows the depth of the crowd; you sense there are far more people present than the frame can show. The dark majority is dignified and calm, not menacing — bystanders, not threats.

Harsh chiaroscuro lighting: a single hard white key light from directly above the front cluster creates a tight pool of brilliance with a smooth, fast falloff into engulfing near-black shadow that fills most of the frame. Faint cold-blue ambient rim light traces the unseen crowd. A trace of cinematic haze hangs in the spotlight beam. Mood is sober, grave, documentary — somber editorial gravity, never glamorous or celebratory. Shot on 35mm film, fine film grain, ultra-realistic skin and fabric textures, very high contrast, premium editorial photography aesthetic. The carrying contrast is hard white light versus engulfing darkness — the small group that was shown against the vast crowd left in the dark.
