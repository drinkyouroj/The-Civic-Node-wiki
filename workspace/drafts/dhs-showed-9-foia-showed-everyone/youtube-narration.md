## Format note (read me first)

**Beat-segmented constant-motion format.** Each `[SCENE NN]` marker is a *scene*; inside it the narration is broken into **one-clause beats** — one short spoken clause + the single visual element that lands on that beat + a `[stop]`. The slideshow skill renders **one slide per beat**, so the beat count is the motion budget: a visual change every ~2-3 seconds.

**Three evolving motifs carry the motion** (each `element:` note names the motif's *next state*, not a fresh card):
- **The invoice → the sealed receipt** — the bill that grows, then becomes the locked evidence cabinet, then the refrain.
- **The list vs. the crowd** — DHS's 335 named (a short lit list) against the 3,789 arrested (a faded crowd); paid off as 9% lit / 91% dark.
- **Renée Good's windshield → the car in custody** — planted at the human cost, paid off when the FBI takes the car.

**Division of labor:** this script owns the beat boundaries, the stops, and *what state* each motif is in. The slideshow owns *how* it moves (fade, count-up, wipe).
