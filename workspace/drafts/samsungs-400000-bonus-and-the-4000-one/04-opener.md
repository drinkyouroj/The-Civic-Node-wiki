# Opener Variants: *Samsung's $400,000 Bonus, and the $4,000 One*

**Template:** Triple Connection | **Trigger:** Unexpected Connection | **Techniques:** A = Mid-Action Drop, B = Counterintuitive Fact

---

### Variant A — Mid-Action Drop

Two workers leave the same Samsung plant in Pyeongtaek at the end of the same shift. They worked it a hundred yards apart. One builds memory chips; this year his bonus runs toward $400,000. The other builds phones and televisions; his comes to about $4,000. Same company. Same shift. A hundred to one.

That gap is the deal Samsung's chip workers ratified on May 27: 10.5% of their division's profit, paid out every year for a decade. They won it at the one spot in the AI economy where the people doing the work had a hand on the valve. Bloomberg calls the winners the "AI aristocrats." The chip worker got his cut because he could choke the supply the whole boom depends on. Most people are standing somewhere with no valve to grab.

**Contract:** the two workers at the gate, and the "valve" (who has a hand on it). The close returns to the two workers as the two chokepoints — the $4,000 worker reframed as the reader/ratepayer, standing where there's no valve to grab.

---

### Variant B — Counterintuitive Fact

Samsung paid some of its memory-chip workers a 2026 bonus of roughly $400,000. It paid workers in the same company who build its phones and televisions about $4,000. Not a typo. The same employer, the same boom, a hundred to one.

The $400,000 came from a deal the chip workers ratified on May 27: a standing claim on 10.5% of their division's profit, every year for the next ten. They could win it because memory is a chokepoint and they had a hand on it; a walkout in Pyeongtaek would have stalled the AI buildout worldwide. Bloomberg calls them the "AI aristocrats." Everyone the same boom reached without a chokepoint to hold is the rest of this story.

**Contract:** the bare $400K/$4K fact and "a chokepoint to hold." The close returns to the people the boom reached who had no chokepoint — the ratepayer as the $4,000 worker with no valve.

A also plants the "valve" image that carries the asymmetry and gives the close its strongest callback. Pick B for the colder, faster, fact-first register.
