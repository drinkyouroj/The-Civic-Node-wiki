## Flagged — recommend fixing before publish (1)

### Claim: "The division's profit had jumped 755% in a single year" (§1)
**Article says:** "A walkout in Pyeongtaek doesn't dent one company's quarter… **The division's profit had jumped 755% in a single year.**"
**Source (Bloomberg/Thorbecke, N83CM) says:** "**The company** reported a staggering 755% jump in profit **during the March quarter**."
**Corroborating source (Reuters raw, "Samsung Flags Eightfold Jump in Q1 Profit"):** company-wide Q1 operating profit, ~8× ≈ +755%.
**Discrepancy — two parts:**
- **Unit:** the 755% is **Samsung Electronics company-wide**, not the DS/semiconductor division specifically. (The division drives almost all of it — the jshgC chart shows DS at 310 of ~330 trillion won, ~94% of profit — so the claim is *directionally* true, but the cited 755% is the company aggregate, not a division-specific figure.)
- **Period:** the 755% is a **March-quarter year-over-year** jump, not a full-year figure. "In a single year" reads as annual; a reader could infer full-year 2025→2026, which the source doesn't support.
**Recommendation:** tighten to **"Samsung's profit had jumped 755% year over year"** (or, for full precision, "Samsung's first-quarter profit had jumped 755% from a year earlier"). This keeps the §1 setup ("doesn't dent one company's quarter…") intact and makes the figure airtight. Minimal, voice-preserving.

## Verified-true, but the inline link doesn't carry the claim (2 — optional sourcing upgrades)

These are **not factual errors** — each claim is true and is supported by a source the article already cites elsewhere. Flagging for airtight sourcing only.

### 1. "exactly one mill in the United States" (sole US GOES producer) — §2
**Article says:** "the steel a transformer's core needs, grain-oriented electrical steel, comes from [exactly one mill in the United States](utilitydive…/723363/), Cleveland-Cliffs, in Butler, Pennsylvania."
**Problem:** the linked **Utility Dive** article supports *Butler, PA* + the Goncalves quote, but **does not state the exclusivity** ("only U.S. mill"). Live re-fetch confirms: "the article contains no statement supporting… monopoly status in GOES production."
**The claim is TRUE:** AIST and Cleveland-Cliffs both confirm Butler Works is "**the only domestic producer of high-silicon grain-oriented electrical steel (GOES)**" and "the only producer of Regular Grain-Oriented (RGO) products in the United States."
**Recommendation:** keep the Utility Dive link for the quote; add a primary source on the exclusivity phrase — [Cleveland-Cliffs Butler Works operations page](https://www.clevelandcliffs.com/operations/steelmaking/butler-works) (Tier 1) or the [AIST Butler Works page](https://www.aist.org/conferences-expositions/aistech/schedule/cleveland-cliffs-butler-works). This is red-flag #1 territory (a link that doesn't contain its claim), mitigated only because the fact is well-documented elsewhere — worth a 30-second fix.

### 2. "SK Hynix's union had pried loose 10% of operating profit the previous fall" (§1)
**Article says:** linked on "[10% of operating profit](en.sedaily…sk-hynix)."
**Problem:** the linked **Seoul Economic Daily** page supports the **10% figure** but states it as SK Hynix policy — it does **not** date it or attribute it to a union win. The "**the previous fall**" timing and the "**union pried loose**" causation are not in the linked source.
**Both are TRUE and now verified live:** SK Hynix's labor union **voted to accept the 10%-of-operating-profit deal on September 4, 2025** (tentative agreement Sept 1), removing the prior 1,000%-of-base-salary cap (Bloomberg, "SK Hynix Set to Pay $2.7 Billion Bonuses," 2025-09-04; Tom's Hardware). September 2025 = "the previous fall" relative to Samsung's May 2026 deal. ✓ And the Bloomberg/Thorbecke piece already cited in the draft (N83CM) explicitly says SK Hynix "**under union pressure, agreed to allocate 10% of operating profits**." So the causation is covered by a source the article uses.
**Recommendation:** optional — the claim is fully sound and the timing/causation are covered by sources in the piece. For belt-and-suspenders, could add the Sept-2025 Bloomberg or Tom's Hardware cite on "the previous fall." Not a blocker.
