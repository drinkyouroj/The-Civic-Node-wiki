A pattern across four federal domains.

CISA ran a program it internally called "switchboarding," flagging social media content to platforms that acted on it under their own policies. Director Jen Easterly defined the information environment as "cognitive infrastructure" within the agency's cybersecurity mandate. Murthy v. Missouri reached the Supreme Court in June 2024 and was dismissed on standing; the constitutional question went unaddressed. The March 2026 settlement bars CISA, the Surgeon General, and the CDC from jawboning private platforms for 10 years. Other federal agencies with equivalent regulatory leverage over the same platforms keep the instrument.

Nineteen states have passed online ID-check laws requiring residents to verify age with a driver's license or face scan; the federal SCREEN Act would extend this nationally. The private verification vendor holds the biometric database. The Tea app breach produced selfies and driver's licenses on 4chan, with address data used to pinpoint home locations. National security letters reach a vendor-held database without the judicial sign-off a subpoena requires.

The FTC found that BetterHelp transferred therapy intake data to advertising platforms relabeled as a behavioral interest category, and ordered $7.8 million in consumer refunds. HIPAA covers the intake questionnaire. The targeting category assembled from that data carries no equivalent statutory protection.

Atlanta's open-records audit, released in late 2025, documents 3,254 Border Patrol searches and 3,383 external immigration-keyword searches of Atlanta Police Department's Flock cameras during a year the city's sanctuary policy was in effect. The Gibbs Mura class action (filed April 3, 2026 in San Francisco Superior Court) documents 1.6 million outside-agency accesses to SFPD's Flock data over seven months; California Civil Code §1798.90.55(b) sets statutory damages at $2,500 per violation. Roughly 4,500 credentialed agencies operate on the Flock network.

No federal court has held that a government-contracted private intermediary must comply with the constitutional limits that would apply if government performed the function directly. Murthy was dismissed on standing. Oregon SB 1516 and California Civil Code §1798.90.55(b) are real remedies; each applies within one state.

Full analysis: https://drinkyouroj.substack.com/p/the-bill-of-rights-ends-at-the-contractors-door
