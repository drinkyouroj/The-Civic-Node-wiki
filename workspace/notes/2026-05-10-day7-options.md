### Option B — Footnote: Transformer lead times

Large power transformers: 128-week average lead time in Q2 2025. Substation transformers stretched to 160 weeks in 2026. Wood Mackenzie projects a 30% supply deficit against demand that's up 274% for generator step-up units since 2019.

The $400 billion AI capex buildout runs through that order book. This week's piece names six reasons why.

### Option C — Compressed Frame: Grid position as the asset

Microsoft took over the Stargate Abilene site for what was already in the ground: 1.2 GW of grid connection. Crusoe is building on-site generation on the same campus to route around a utility queue that won't move.

Two moves, same shape: hyperscalers acquiring time someone else already cleared. This week's piece names the pattern at scale.
