## NOTE 1: Framework Hand (3–5 PM)

*Analytical tool: how to read a CPI report when a supply shock is active.*
*Source: BLS April CPI, May 12. Pre-verified in wiki.*
*No image needed.*

---

The April CPI print has a mechanism inside it.

Energy: +3.8% for the month. The Strait of Hormuz was closed for most of April.
That's roughly 40% of the full headline increase, and it traces directly to throughput,
not to monetary conditions.

The useful read for any month the Strait is closed: start with the energy line, map it
against the Hormuz calendar, then read the rest of the table. The mechanism is in the
component, not the headline.
