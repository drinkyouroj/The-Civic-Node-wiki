## NOTE 2: Stress Test (6–7 PM)

*Name the assumption. Test the mechanism. Return the verdict.*
*No image needed.*

---

Stress-testing the hawkish Warsh trade: higher rates, tighter conditions, lower inflation.

The mechanism: rate hikes reach demand-pull inflation. A wage-price spiral, domestic demand
running hot against constrained supply — those are conditions rate increases address.

The constraint inventory entering June 16-17: Hormuz throughput (95% of pre-war traffic not
transiting) and the tariff structure (19.7% effective rate, highest since Smoot-Hawley). Neither
is demand-pull. A hawkish chair with two active supply shocks in the data is not a
lower-inflation outcome. It's a recession bet on top of an existing supply disruption.

The trade only works if the inflation is demand-driven. The primary sources say it isn't.

*~95 words. Verdict in the last two sentences.*
