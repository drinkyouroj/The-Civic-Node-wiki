### Option A — Direct operator observation

I run Helium hotspots.

On August 1 2025, the network reset all veHNT delegations that had been made before July 1. To stay eligible for rewards after that date, an operator had to do two things: re-delegate, and assign a proxy as a backup.

If you only did the first one, you lost reward eligibility. If you only did the second one, you lost reward eligibility. The hardware kept running. The coverage kept being provided. The earnings went to zero.

The Helium Foundation's own explainer on the halving recommends operators "set a proxy as a backup to ensure you don't miss out on rewards." Operators run the hardware. The Foundation recommends they hand the vote to someone else.
