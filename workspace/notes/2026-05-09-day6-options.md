## NOTE 2: Framework Hand — Seed for AI's Power Bill (9:30–10 PM)

*Plants the analytical move for the May 15 piece on AI grid capacity. No link. The issue isn't live yet.*

---

### Option A — "The one question for AI buildout coverage"

When you see an AI data center announcement, ask one question before you read the capex number: where does the power come from?

Chips get the headlines. Grid capacity is what decides which data centers actually turn on, and it takes seven years to expand. The chip layer and the grid layer are on different timelines.

*~56 words*

---

### Option B — Two documents

The AI buildout story is being told from the chip layer.

The grid layer is bigger, more concentrated, and slower. Power purchase agreements signed in late 2025 have lead times of five to seven years. The chip timeline doesn't bind the buildout the way the grid timeline does.

Next week I'm writing about the power bill.

*~58 words*

---

### Option C — Two documents, structural contrast

When an AI company announces a new data center, two documents matter.

The press release names the capex. The power purchase agreement names when the building actually turns on. Most coverage reads the first one. The second has the timeline that binds.

*~46 words*

---

**Recommendation:** Option C. Cleanest structure — two documents, no setup required, earns a restack from anyone who covers energy or AI infrastructure. Option A works better if you want to plant the explicit "ask this question" frame that the Framework Hand format is built for.
