**4/**
The federal funds rate works on two things: demand-pull inflation and inflation expectations. Raise rates, demand cools.

Supply-shock inflation is different. The cause is physical scarcity. You can't raise interest rates to reopen a shipping lane.

*~249 chars*

**5/**
History helps here, but only if you read it correctly.

1973: oil spiked ~400%. Volcker took rates to ~22%. That broke inflation expectations. It didn't break the oil shock. Oil fell later, for separate reasons.

Nobody is doing 22% rates against the Strait.
