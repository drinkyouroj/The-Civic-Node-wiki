[02] FRAME
THREE FRAMES

The first two are the ones every analyst reaches for.

[01]

Stock-flow.

The Treasury sells, the market clears, the price moves. Works for cash in normal weeks. Not this week.

[02]

Convexity.

Dealers absorb whatever stock-flow can't, at a cost that compounds with duration. We've seen this.

[03]

Settlement.

The pipes themselves have capacity. Sovereign issuance can cross dealer balance-sheet limits.

[02] FRAME · COMPARE

Two stories. Only one is being told in public.

STORY A · MONEY
"It's a payments innovation."

Stablecoin issuance is a payments-rails story. Central bank concern, central bank lane.

STORY B · DEBT
"It's who finances the deficit."

Issuers are net buyers of short-dated Treasuries. Treasury concern, Treasury lane.
