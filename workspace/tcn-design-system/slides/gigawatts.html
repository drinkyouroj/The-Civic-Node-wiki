THREE CALENDARS · ONE PROJECT

Different units. Different timelines. Only one of them gates anything.

[01] QUEUE

4.5 years.

US median wait for interconnection — 2024 completions. Seven in Northern Virginia, nine-plus in California. 77% of the 2000–2019 cohort withdrew before energization.

[02] TRANSFORMERS

128 weeks.

Average lead time on large power transformers, Q2 2025. Generator step-up units: 144 weeks. Demand up 274% since 2019. Wood Mackenzie projects a 30% supply deficit.

[03] SUBSTATION

7–10 years.

Permitting and siting on new transmission, per Microsoft's own January 2026 framing. Transmission permitting takes the time it takes. The longest calendar wins.

The chip layer reverts in 18 months. The grid layer reverts in 7 years.

CHIP LAYER · UNCONCENTRATES BY 2028
"What fits in an earnings call."

CXMT's Shanghai mega-fab and YMTC's third Wuhan facility both target 2027. Competitive pricing pressure lands 2028 at earliest. The press release is denominated in dollars. This is the layer the market reads.

GRID LAYER · IF EVERYTHING BREAKS RIGHT
"What fits in a PUC filing."

Transformer manufacturers don't reach 9,000 units/year by 2030 from 1,500 today without the gap widening first. The queue can't be reformed into compliance with five-year financing horizons. Different document class.
