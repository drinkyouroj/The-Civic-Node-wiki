[01] CONTEXT · APR 22, 2026

A 21-mile waterway controlled by a country we've been at war with since February 28.

Brent crude crossed $100 a barrel on April 22, after the IRGC fired on three ships and seized two. US inflation sits at 3.3% — well above the 2% target. [02] FRAME · THE TOOL THAT DOESN'T FIT
THREE INFLATIONS · ONE COMMITTEE

The Fed has rates. The other two it doesn't.

[01]

Demand-pull.

Too much money chasing too few goods. What the federal funds rate is calibrated for. The Fed slows the economy until demand cools.

[02]

Tariff shock.

Effective tariff rate: 19.7% — the highest since 1933. Created by executive action. Removed by executive action. The Fed cannot.

[03]

Chokepoint shock.

Iran charges up to $2M per ship in yuan and stablecoins. A tax in a currency the FOMC does not set the rate for, on a flow it does not control.

[02] FRAME · HISTORICAL READING

Volcker won the demand battle. The supply problem resolved on its own.

1973 · OPEC EMBARGO
"22% rates broke expectations."

Oil up ~400%. A decade later, Volcker pushed the federal funds rate to ~22% in mid-1981. That crushed demand and broke expectations. Oil fell separately — new supply, demand destruction, weakened OPEC discipline.

2026 · STRAIT CLOSURE
"Nobody is doing 22% against Hormuz."

Two concurrent supply shocks, different political origins, different resolution paths. The same president pressuring the Fed to cut is also the source of one of them. No historical chair-environment combination maps to this.
