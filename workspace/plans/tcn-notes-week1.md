Reading the medical code on a single claim takes longer than that. Nobody read yours. Nobody was going to read it unless you appealed.

### Note 2 — FH

There's a class of civil lawsuit where winning is irrelevant to the strategy.

Discovery is expensive. Depositions are expensive. Being publicly accused of something for fourteen months costs in ways no invoice captures. The verdict arrives in a different news cycle than the accusation. The correction doesn't catch up.

## DAY 5

### Note 1 — ST
*Source: Cheaper AI Won't Use Less of Anything*

After DeepSeek, Satya Nadella invoked Jevons paradox to reassure investors. His point: cheaper AI drives more AI demand, so the stocks would be fine.

The mechanism he cited is real. His application of it was selective.

Jevons fires when three conditions hold: the technology has elastic demand, the efficiency savings get recycled into more consumption, and there are no structural limits on expansion. Nadella asserted all three. He didn't demonstrate any of them.

The counterexample is agriculture. Tractors made farming 20x more efficient. Farming went from 40% of the US workforce in 1900 to under 2% today. Demand didn't scale to absorb what the machines could produce. The efficiency gain went through labor instead.

Whether AI looks more like refrigerators or tractors determines whether your job survives. Nadella has an obvious preference for which answer investors hear.

### Note 2 — FH
*Source: The System Is Functioning Correctly — cross-domain synthesis*

The same four-part architecture appears in every accountability-evasion case, regardless of institution.

Evidence custody: whoever controls the investigation of their own wrongdoing controls whether it's investigated. Procedural substitution: replace the investigation with an investigation-shaped object. Exhaustion as exit condition: run the clock until the press or the legal deadline gives up. Toothless laws: rules that demand accountability with no way to enforce them.

Cigna's denial system runs components 1 and 3. The Epstein release runs all four. Metro Surge ran 1, 2, and 4.

The institution changes. The components don't. Once you see the architecture, you stop being surprised by the outcomes.
