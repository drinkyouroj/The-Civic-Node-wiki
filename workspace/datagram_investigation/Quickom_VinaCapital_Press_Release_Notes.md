## Key Facts

**The investment:**
- VinaCapital Ventures led a **USD $1.5 million seed funding round** in Quickom
- Announced March 6, 2024 — approximately 8 months before the Datagram TGE (November 18, 2025) and roughly 15 months before Jason Brink's departure (January 17, 2026)
- VinaCapital Ventures is the technology investment vehicle of VinaCapital Group

**The company:**
- **Quickom** — Vietnam-based video conferencing provider
- Founder and CEO: **Dr. William H. Nguyen** — described as "a highly regarded Vietnamese technology pioneer and serial entrepreneur"
- Founded: 2021
- Holds three U.S. patents in communication technology
- Headquartered (implied): Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

**The technology (per press release):**
> "Quickom's solution differs from other conferencing providers by utilizing a decentralized peer-hosted network architecture that enables data transmission directly between users or nodes, bypassing the need for a centralized data center. The decentralized network employs an algorithm that extracts unused bandwidth and processing power to process and route video, audio and other data, significantly bringing down the overall costs associated with hosting virtual calls."

This is **identical language** to the Datagram pitch. Datagram was the Web3/node-sale layer built on top of Quickom's core technology.

**Pre-existing enterprise traction (claimed):**
- Deployed with "prominent domestic and international enterprises as well as multiple Vietnamese government agencies"
- Hosted 500+ livestreaming events in 2023
- Named clients/partners: Vietnam National Innovation Center (NIC), Techfest Vietnam, HOZO, ETH Vietnam
- Subscription video conferencing service already live

**The Web3 pivot (already underway at time of press release):**
- Chat3 — described as a "SocialFi" platform launched ~4 months before March 2024 (i.e., approximately November 2023)
- Chat3 had already received funding from **Arche Fund** (Web3 VC, Southeast Asia) and a grant from **Mantle** (blockchain backed by Mirana Ventures and Bybit Exchange)
- Quickom described as "leveraging and innovating its current technology for the Web3 segment"

**Significance**: The Web3 pivot was already in motion before VinaCapital's seed investment. Datagram and its node-sale structure came later — but the groundwork was laid here.

**On "Yu te Han":** No public records found for this alternate name. Chris may have been referring to a legal/Vietnamese name or misremembered. "Richard Han" is the confirmed public identity.

**Why he's not in the press release:** Hoang Duc Trung is the Partner who publicly fronts the Quickom deal. Richard Han, as VP/investment manager, would typically be the deal-level person — doing due diligence, managing the portfolio company relationship, attending board meetings — while the Partner takes the public quote. This structure is entirely consistent with Chris's description of Richard as the hands-on "handler" who controlled day-to-day decisions.

### 2. The corporate structure: Quickom → Datagram
This press release establishes the lineage clearly. VinaCapital invested in **Quickom** (the underlying tech company). Datagram Network appears to have been the Web3/token layer built on top of Quickom's infrastructure — likely a separate legal entity. This means:
- VinaCapital's investment was in Quickom, not Datagram per se
- The node sale, DGRAM tokens, and community were a separate structure
- The question of how Datagram's treasury related to Quickom's equity is now a live investigative question

### 3. Enterprise client claims vs. reality
The press release claims Quickom had enterprise traction, government clients, and 500+ livestreaming events before the VinaCapital investment. Jason's departure statement said "several core representations we relied upon, including reported user adoption and project readiness, could not be substantiated by verifiable data or observable on chain activity." Were those unverifiable representations the same ones made to VinaCapital in 2024?

### 4. Chat3 and the multiple-project problem
Kyle mentions in the Jan 2, 2026 meeting that William was "using forty percent of dev resources to build out an AI that judges your accent" and building "non-Datagram shit." Chat3 predates Datagram's node sale by over a year. Was William dividing his engineering resources between Quickom, Chat3, and Datagram simultaneously? This could explain why Datagram's product never shipped.

### 5. VinaCapital's current position
VinaCapital is a publicly accountable firm (LSE-listed fund). If Datagram/Quickom collapsed under circumstances suggesting fraud or misrepresentation, they may have their own legal exposure. Their silence since January 2026 is notable. Worth checking: has VinaCapital made any statement about Quickom or Datagram since Jason's departure?
