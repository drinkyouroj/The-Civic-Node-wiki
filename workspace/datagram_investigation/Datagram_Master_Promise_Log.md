## EVERGREEN / STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

These claims were made repeatedly across multiple sources throughout the project's public history, without meaningful verification:

### Network Scale (Repeated in every major AMA and press appearance)
- **"180,000+ nodes"** running on the Datagram network
- **"1.2 million users"** on the network
- **"200+ enterprises"** using or "trusting" Datagram
- **"400 companies, couple million users in Southeast Asia"** (later, inflated version)
- Source verification: None provided in any public-facing material. Kyle meeting does not validate these figures.

### Product Claims (Repeated throughout)
- Core Substrate is a live, production B2B product
- Tri-token model is operational
- AI inference will be available to node operators (disabled on testnet; never re-enabled publicly)
- No mobile nodes — ever (consistent and firm)
- Burn-and-mint equilibrium creates deflationary pressure over time

### Tokenomics (Structural)
- 10B max DGRAM supply
- 0.125%/day emission decay
- Data Credits: non-transferable, burned DGRAM to acquire
- Service tokens (UDP/TCP/AI) auto-convert daily to DGRAM

### Node Sale (Structural)
- 25,000 Full Core Nodes, Year 1
- $150–$750 price range
- ERC-721 NFT, locked 1 year to wallet from mainnet date
- After Year 1: up to 75,000 additional nodes via staking only
- Reward split: 80% uptime, 20% usage

### Capsule NFT (Structural)
- 10% immediate vest at TGE
- 90% vested over 12 months from TGE
- Testnet reward container (not purchasable)

### ⚠️ Scale Claim Escalation Pattern

Network figures grew over time without verification milestones:
- Early 2025: "180,000+ nodes," "1.2M users," "200+ enterprises"
- Mid 2025 (Catching Up AMA): "400 companies, couple million users in Southeast Asia"
- Bitcoin.com News interview: same inflated figures presented to press as fact
- Kyle meeting (Jan 2026): no validation or acknowledgment of these figures

### ⚠️ 80% "Dead Accounts" Capsule Justification

At TGE celebration stream, the team used the claim that 80% of wallets were "dead" at capsule claim time to justify vesting mechanics design. This retroactively reframes a potential technical/design failure as intentional. No independent verification of this figure was provided.

### ⚠️ US Regulatory Constraint

Repeated claim that the team cannot advertise to US citizens due to regulatory environment — yet node sales were conducted without apparent geographic KYC exclusions. The extent to which this was enforced versus used as a marketing limitation excuse is unclear.
