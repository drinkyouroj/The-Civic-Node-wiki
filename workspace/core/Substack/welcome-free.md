## Body

You're subscribed. Here's what that means.

**The Civic Node** is long-form analysis at the intersection of monetary mechanics, political systems, and decentralized infrastructure. Each issue takes one connection apart: the footnotes that contradict the headline, the structural reason an institution behaved the way it did, the pattern running beneath three stories that don't usually share a page.

One thesis, argued through. Not a digest. Not a roundup.

A few things I commit to upfront:

**No sponsors.** The newsletter has never run a sponsored issue.

**No token positions. No advisory roles. No allocation letters.** If I write about a project I could plausibly be incentivized about, I'll say so explicitly — in the piece, before the analysis.

**No team jerseys.** The analysis is structural, not partisan. I hold that line publicly and without apology.

The standard you should hold me to: if you've been thinking about something for two weeks and I name it with the structural reason attached, that's the piece working. If I'm telling you something you already knew, or something completely alien — I missed. Tell me.
