# Paid Subscription Renewal Email

## Subject line

Your Civic Node subscription renews [date].

---

## Body

Your paid subscription renews on [date] at $[price]/year.

If you need to cancel before then: [link].

If you have questions, reply here.

The archive is at [link] — everything published since you subscribed is there.

Thanks for staying on the paid list. It's what makes the research possible.

— Justin Hearn
The Civic Node

---

## Notes

- This email has one job: inform. Not persuade. They already paid; don't re-sell them.
- The cancel link goes first, before the archive link — transparency is the brand. A subscriber who sees the cancel option prominently and doesn't use it is more loyal than one who couldn't find it.
- "Thanks for staying on the paid list. It's what makes the research possible." — brief, genuine, not effusive. Connects their payment to something concrete (research infrastructure) rather than abstract gratitude.
- Keep the body under 75 words. This is a transactional email. The voice is in the restraint.
