## Notes

- No guilt-trip. No "we miss you." No countdown timer.

- The free-tier affirmation ("the analysis doesn't change") is genuine and worth stating. It's also good for retention: it tells a lapsed subscriber that leaving the paid tier isn't a punishment.
- One CTA: renew link. No secondary asks.
