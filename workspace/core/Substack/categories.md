# Substack Categories

## Recommendation: Finance + Technology

**Keep:** Finance
**Swap "World Politics" for:** Technology

**Rationale:**

Finance covers the monetary mechanics pillar — Fed dynamics, petrodollar recycling, fiscal dominance, macro. That's right.

Technology better maps to the DePIN/decentralized infrastructure pillar and the AI/compute coverage — the content that is most structurally distinctive and most likely to attract the crypto-native operator segment browsing Substack by category. It also signals the operating-credibility angle ("ran hardware") that differentiates the publication from armchair analysts covering adjacent topics.

Politics is the most overcrowded Substack category. The political-systems reader who would subscribe to this newsletter doesn't discover it through Substack browse — they arrive via X or a trusted recommendation. The brand doc is explicit: "The audience does not discover via Substack recommendations, directories, or paid promotion." So the category choice is more about how the publication reads when someone lands on it than about algorithmic discovery. Finance + Technology frames the publication as cross-domain analytical, not political commentary.

The political-systems content is a pillar, not the category. The category should signal the distinctive lane, which is the intersection of money and technical infrastructure.
