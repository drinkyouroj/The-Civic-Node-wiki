## Notes

- This email has one job the free-subscriber welcome doesn't: give the reader a clear off-ramp without friction. They didn't choose to subscribe in the same way an organic subscriber did. Acknowledge that.
- Customize the [bracketed] section for each import context — different list sources warrant different one-line explanations.
- "I don't do cross-promotion swaps or list trades" is reassuring to an imported subscriber who may wonder why they're suddenly on a newsletter they don't remember signing up for.
- The archive link is more important here than in the free welcome — they need to evaluate before trusting.
