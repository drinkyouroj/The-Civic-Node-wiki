## Full Value Proposition

I help systems thinkers — engineers, operators, and insiders who've learned not to trust official explanations — achieve composed clarity about how power actually works, by providing primary-source analysis that names the connection across monetary mechanics, decentralized infrastructure, and political systems before the discourse catches up. Every piece delivers at least one analytical move the reader wasn't fully making themselves. If a piece doesn't name a connection, expose a mechanism, or stress-test a claim against its primary sources, it doesn't ship.

---

## Short Form

The Civic Node: for people who've noticed that the writers who understand money don't engage with power, and the writers who cover power don't engage with crypto. Analysis that names the play across all three — without a team jersey.

---

## Editorial Filter (derived from the full VP)

Every piece must do at least one of these three things:
- Name a connection the reader wasn't fully making themselves
- Expose a mechanism by tracing it to its primary source
- Hold a position and defend it — not "it's complicated"

If a piece doesn't clear at least one bar, it doesn't ship.
